Garland's Late Three-Point Play Lifts Clippers Past Thunder 113-112

Garland's driving three-point play capped a late 7-0 run as the Clippers stunned the short-handed Thunder.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 14, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Darius Garland delivered the biggest shot of the Los Angeles Clippers' season on Friday night, completing a three-point play with 50.4 seconds left to lift his team past the Oklahoma City Thunder 113-112.

The Clippers trailed 112-106 with 2:43 to play before closing the game on a 7-0 run. Garland's driving basket, plus the foul, gave Los Angeles its first lead of the closing stretch and proved to be the winner.

Brook Lopez led the Clippers with a team-high 26 points. Garland finished with 24 points. Derrick Jones Jr. and rookie Keaton Wagler scored 14 points each.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander was brilliant in defeat, scoring 40 points on 15-of-26 shooting, including 2-of-4 from three, with five assists, four rebounds and a steal. His stepback 21-footer over Jones Jr. at the buzzer missed, ending Oklahoma City's last chance to steal the win.

Jared McCain gave the Thunder a lift with 23 points, 17 of them in the third quarter, when he went 7-of-11 from three-point range. Isaiah Hartenstein added 21 points and 16 rebounds, but the Thunder could not hold the lead they built in the second half.

Oklahoma City outscored Los Angeles 40-25 in the third quarter to take control, but the Clippers kept the game within reach and found a way in the final minutes. The Thunder were badly short-handed, playing without Chet Holmgren with a knee injury, Jalen Williams after leg and ankle surgery, Cason Wallace with a shoulder injury, Jaylin Williams with a back injury and Ajay Mitchell with an ankle injury.

The loss was Oklahoma City's third in a row, dropping the defending champions to 8-5. The Clippers, who entered the night at 2-8, have now won two of their last three games after a 1-7 start, and both teams are 1-1 in NBA Cup play.

The night also marked the season debut of Nikola Topić, who played 14 minutes with four rebounds, three assists and two steals while going 0-of-4 from the field. Topić has overcome a partially torn ACL, chemotherapy for testicular cancer and back surgery since being drafted, making his return one of the feel-good stories of the night.

The win was the Clippers' second in three games, and it came against the best team in the league, short-handed or not. Los Angeles started the season 1-7 and looked like a team headed for a long winter, but the last three games suggest a team that has found something. Garland's shot will be remembered, but the comeback from six down in the final minutes was a team effort.

For Oklahoma City, the loss was the third in a row, and the injury list is the worry. A team can survive a bad night, but it cannot survive losing five rotation players for long. The Thunder's depth has been their strength, and it is being tested now. Sunday's trip to Sacramento is a chance to stop the slide before it becomes a real problem.

Oklahoma City now travels to Sacramento to face the Kings on Sunday at 8 p.m. Central time. The Thunder need a win to stop the slide, while the Clippers will try to build on a victory that showed they can beat the best team in the league even when they are far from full strength. Read the report from Reuters

Why it matters: The result matters more for what it says about both teams than for the Cup standings: the Clippers, written off after a 1-7 start, just beat the defending champions at home in Inglewood, while Oklahoma City has now lost three straight and is running out of healthy bodies. Garland's late heroics give Los Angeles something to build on, but the Thunder's injury list is the real story, if Holmgren and Williams stay out much longer, the three-game slide could become something worse.
