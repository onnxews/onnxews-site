Newly Crowned Champion Antonelli Tops Practice at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli went fastest in second practice at Interlagos, edging Lando Norris after the McLaren driver had led the opening session.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Kimi Antonelli set the fastest time in second practice for the Sao Paulo Grand Prix on Friday, underlining that becoming Formula 1 world champion has not slowed him down. The newly crowned champion recorded a 1 minute 10.763 seconds lap on soft tires at Interlagos to finish 0.253 seconds clear of McLaren's Lando Norris, reversing the order from the opening session earlier in the day.

Norris had topped first practice with a 1 minute 11.717 seconds lap on soft tires, finishing just 0.082 seconds ahead of Antonelli, with Max Verstappen completing the top three for Red Bull. The opening session was interrupted by a red flag after Freddie Slater's Audi stopped on track between Turns 9 and 10, bringing the running to a temporary halt under cloudy skies with rain threatening.

The second session was just as tight at the front. Verstappen finished third in FP2, just 0.006 seconds behind Norris, while George Russell was fourth in the second Mercedes, a further 0.014 seconds adrift. Four cars covered by less than three tenths of a second promises a fiercely contested qualifying session, and the margins suggest that the smallest mistake will cost places on the grid.

Lewis Hamilton was sixth for Ferrari, 0.450 seconds off Antonelli's pace, while home favorite Gabriel Bortoleto claimed eighth for Audi to secure another top-ten result in front of the Brazilian crowd, ahead of Isack Hadjar and Oliver Bearman. Bortoleto's performance brought the biggest cheer of the day from the Interlagos grandstands.

Antonelli's weekend comes days after he became Formula 1's youngest-ever world champion at the age of 20, a milestone that capped a remarkable rise through the junior ranks and into a Mercedes race seat. The championship may be decided, but the 20-year-old is showing no sign of easing off, and his FP2 time was the fastest lap of the day by a quarter of a second.

Seven rookies made appearances across the two sessions, giving teams a chance to evaluate young talent while the regular drivers fine-tuned their setups. The rookie running also added to the traffic and the stoppages, with Slater's stricken Audi the most disruptive moment of the day.

The weather remains the great unknown for the rest of the weekend. Cloudy skies greeted the drivers for the second hour of practice, with reports of light drizzle during the session and increasingly gloomy conditions as the prospect of rain over the weekend drew closer. Interlagos is famous for weather that can change a race in minutes, and a wet qualifying or grand prix would throw the form book out entirely.

Third practice gets underway at 11:30 a.m. local time on Saturday, with qualifying to follow, and the sprint and grand prix format means there is little time to get the setup wrong. Antonelli and Norris have split the practice sessions between them, Verstappen is lurking within a few hundredths, and the championship may be over but the racing is not.

For Antonelli, the weekend is a victory lap with a point to prove. A win in Brazil would be the perfect way to celebrate a first world title, and on Friday's evidence, the Mercedes is the car to beat. Norris and Verstappen will have other ideas.
