Grammy Nominations for 2027 Will Be Announced November 16, With Pharrell, Kehlani and Olivia Dean Among the Presenters

Pharrell Williams, Kehlani, Olivia Dean, David Guetta and more will announce the nominees for the 69th Grammys on Monday, November 16. The ceremony takes place February 7, 2027, in Los Angeles.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 9, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

The Recording Academy has announced the star-studded lineup that will reveal the nominees for the 69th Grammy Awards, with the nominations set to be unveiled on Monday, November 16. The announcement will be led by Grammys CEO Harvey Mason Jr. and Chair of the Board of Trustees Dr. Chelsey Green, joined by a roster of Grammy-winning and Grammy-nominated artists.

The presenters announcing the nominations include Alan Menken, Amy Allen, Carly Pearce, David Guetta, FYÜTCH and Aura V, Herbie Hancock, Israel Houghton, Juanes, Kehlani, Linda Perry, Meghan Foley and Michelle Holme, Olivia Dean, Pharrell Williams, Renée Fleming, YUNGBLUD and Ziggy Marley. The mix spans generations and genres, from film-score legend Menken to breakout star Olivia Dean.

The nominations special presentation begins at 7:45 a.m. ET, or 4:45 a.m. PT, on Monday, November 16, and will stream live on Grammy.com and the official Grammys YouTube channel. The announcement will also receive special coverage on ABC's Good Morning America. The complete list of nominees across all 100 Grammy categories will be published on Grammy.com at 9 a.m. ET, along with an official press release.

The 2027 ceremony itself will take place on Sunday, February 7, 2027, live from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. It will air on ABC and stream on Disney+ and Hulu, continuing the show's move to a broadcast-and-streaming simulcast. The ceremony is the biggest night in the music industry's calendar, and the nominations morning is its first major checkpoint.

The eligibility period for the 2027 awards covers music released between August 31, 2025, and August 28, 2026. Only recordings entered for consideration can compete, and first-round voting to determine the nominees ran from October 12 to October 22. Final-round voting to choose the winners runs from December 10, 2026, through January 7, 2027.

This year's nominations carry extra interest because the Academy has added five brand-new categories, taking the total to 100. The new categories reflect the way music is made and consumed now, and the nominations morning will show which artists and recordings the Academy's voters believe defined the eligibility year.

The presenter list itself is a statement about where the Academy wants the show to sit. Pharrell Williams and David Guetta bring global hit-making clout, Herbie Hancock and Renée Fleming bring institutional weight, and Kehlani, Olivia Dean and YUNGBLUD represent the younger generation of artists the Grammys are keen to keep close. Juanes and Israel Houghton underline the show's reach beyond the English-language mainstream.

For the artists hoping to hear their names on November 16, the morning is the culmination of months of campaigning. Labels and managers spend the autumn pushing their releases to the Academy's voting members, and a nomination — in any category — can transform a career, a tour and a streaming catalogue overnight.

The countdown is now a week long. On Monday morning, the presenters will take turns reading out the names across the 100 categories, and by 9 a.m. ET the full list will be public. Until then, the speculation — and the predictions — are the music industry's favorite guessing game.
