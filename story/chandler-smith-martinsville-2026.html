CHANDLER SMITH TAKES MARTINSVILLE AS HONEYCUTT CARRIES 20-POINT LEAD TO THE FINALE

Chandler Smith and the No. 38 Front Row Motorsports team won the Black's Tire 200 at Martinsville Speedway, their third victory of the 2026 NASCAR Truck Series season.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 31, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Chandler Smith and the No. 38 Front Row Motorsports team won the Black's Tire 200 at Martinsville Speedway, their third victory of the 2026 NASCAR Truck Series season.

Smith passed Layne Riggs on the final restart to secure the win, one more victory with the No. 38 team before moving to the NASCAR O'Reilly Series in 2027.

Kaden Honeycutt now carries a 20-point lead over Riggs heading into the championship finale at Homestead-Miami Speedway, with two races left in 2026.

The Martinsville results had Ben Rhodes second and Riggs third, with Honeycutt finishing 14th, setting up the first Chase championship finale in the new format.

The finale at Homestead-Miami will decide the Truck Series crown.
