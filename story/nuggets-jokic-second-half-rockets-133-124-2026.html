Jokic Erupts After Halftime as Nuggets Outlast Rockets 133-124 in NBA Cup Thriller

Nikola Jokic scored 24 of his 26 points after halftime, including 18 in the third quarter, as Denver closed on a late run to beat Houston in a game with 32 lead changes.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 21, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Nikola Jokic had 26 points, 11 assists and seven rebounds as the Denver Nuggets defeated the Houston Rockets 133-124 in a back-and-forth NBA Cup game on Friday night at Ball Arena. Jokic scored 24 of his 26 points after halftime, including 18 in the third quarter, to lift Denver past a Houston team that refused to go away until the final minutes.

The game swung back and forth all night. The two teams combined for 32 lead changes and 17 ties, the most lead changes in an NBA game this season, and neither team led by more than five points until there were just over two minutes remaining. Denver led 62-60 at halftime despite Jokic and Jamal Murray combining for only six points in the first half.

Jokic came alive in the third quarter, scoring 18 points to give the Nuggets a 99-95 lead late in the period. Houston, which got 13 points from Fred VanVleet in the third, answered with a 7-0 run to end the quarter and carried a 102-99 lead into the fourth.

Houston kept pushing early in the fourth. Alperen Sengun's big night spurred the Rockets to a 111-110 lead midway through the period, and a dunk by Amen Thompson followed by a VanVleet jumper pushed the Houston lead to five points with 5:10 remaining.

Denver answered with the decisive burst. The Nuggets scored the next 10 points, capped by a Christian Braun steal and dunk, to take the lead for good. A dunk by Jabari Smith Jr. briefly stopped the momentum, but Murray hit a baseline jumper and Jokic made two free throws to make it 124-117.

Sengun followed with a dunk of his own, but Murray was fouled on a three-point attempt and made all three free throws to restore an eight-point lead with 1:33 left. Kevin Durant hit a short jumper, but Jokic put back his own miss to seal the win as Denver finished the game on a 23-9 run.

Jokic had plenty of help. Braun and Cameron Johnson scored 19 points each, Aaron Gordon added 18, DeMar DeRozan contributed 15 and Murray finished with 14 points and seven assists. Seven Nuggets reached double figures as Denver won its second straight game and its sixth in eight.

Sengun led Houston with a triple-double of 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists, his second triple-double of the season. He played the first quarter in protective goggles after sustaining a scratched cornea in a win on Wednesday, then ditched the glasses before the first quarter ended.

Each of Houston's starters finished with at least 16 points. VanVleet scored 25 and added nine assists, Thompson had 22, Smith finished with 21 and Durant added 16. But the Rockets' backups provided only 12 points and combined to miss 13 of 17 shots, a gap that mattered as the game tightened late.

The result flipped the early meeting between the teams. Houston won the first matchup 110-100 on Nov. 9, just 11 days earlier. Denver improved to 2-0 in NBA Cup West Group A play to match Dallas atop the group standings, while Houston fell to 1-2. The Nuggets next visit the Chicago Bulls on Sunday, while the Rockets host the Los Angeles Clippers on Monday.

Why it matters: This was more than a regular-season win: it was a measuring-stick game in the NBA Cup between two Western Conference contenders, and Denver answered the questions Houston had posed in the first meeting. Jokic's quiet first half followed by a 24-point second half showed why the Nuggets remain so hard to beat when games tighten — even on a night when their star barely touched the ball early, the supporting cast kept them close enough for him to take over. For Houston, the lesson is about depth: five starters scoring 16 or more is a winning formula most nights, but 12 bench points will not hold up in close games against elite teams. As of Nov. 20, Denver sits 2-0 in its Cup group with real momentum, while Houston's 1-2 start leaves little margin for error.
