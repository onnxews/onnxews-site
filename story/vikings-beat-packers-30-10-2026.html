Cousins Stars in Emotional Vikings Return as Minnesota Routs Packers 30-10

Kirk Cousins threw two touchdown passes in his first start back with Minnesota, and the Vikings' defense sacked Jordan Love eight times and forced four turnovers in a 30-10 win over the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 16, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Kirk Cousins came home on Sunday, and the Minnesota Vikings came with him. Just five days after Minnesota reacquired him from the Las Vegas Raiders at the trade deadline, Cousins threw two touchdown passes to lead the Vikings to a 30-10 victory over the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field, snapping a two-game losing streak and completing a season sweep of their NFC North rivals.

Cousins completed 21 of 31 passes for 207 yards and two touchdowns with one interception in his first start for Minnesota since 2023. It was a full-circle afternoon: the last time Cousins had played at Lambeau Field in a Vikings uniform, on Oct. 29, 2023, he tore his Achilles tendon in a 24-10 Minnesota victory, an injury that ended his first stint with the team. Speaking to ESPN after the game, he grew emotional reflecting on the return.

The 38-year-old quarterback wasted no time making a difference. Cousins connected with Myles Price for a 38-yard touchdown in the first quarter and later found rookie tight end Gavin Bartholomew with a 20-yarder in the fourth quarter. Both Price and Bartholomew caught their first career touchdown passes.

Cousins also rekindled his connection with star receiver Justin Jefferson, who caught nine passes for 74 yards, season highs in both catches and targets. With Jordan Addison and tight end T.J. Hockenson sidelined by injuries, Cousins spread the ball around and steadied an offense that had scored 16 points or fewer in each of its previous three games under quarterbacks Kyler Murray and Carson Wentz.

Minnesota's defense was just as decisive as its returning quarterback. The Vikings sacked Packers quarterback Jordan Love eight times, their most in a game since recording nine against Miami in 2018, with Levi Drake Rodriguez accounting for two of them. The pressure never let Green Bay settle, and Love finished 17 of 27 for 220 yards with a touchdown and an interception.

The Vikings also won the turnover battle in emphatic fashion, forcing four turnovers. Green Bay lost three fumbles, each of which led to a Minnesota field goal, and Love was intercepted in the end zone by Harrison Smith. The Packers had entered the game with a plus-8 turnover margin that ranked second in the NFL, but virtually nothing went right for them on Sunday.

Minnesota never looked threatened. The Vikings had scored 49 consecutive points against Green Bay, stretching back to their 39-22 season-opening win in Week 1, before Love's 32-yard touchdown pass to J. Michael Sturdivant cut the lead to 20-7 in the third quarter. It was the most points Minnesota had scored since that Week 1 meeting with the Packers.

The win improved the Vikings to 7-2 and dropped the Packers to 6-4, with Minnesota beating Green Bay for the fifth time in the last six meetings between the teams. Packers coach Matt LaFleur was blunt afterward, saying his team had been humbled, while Vikings coach Kevin O'Connell praised Cousins for running the show with poise and leadership in his first week back. Read the Associated Press report on Cousins' return

Cousins himself kept the moment in perspective after the game. He said there was still plenty of football ahead and that the story of his second stint in Minnesota would be written much later, but he called it a blessing to come back to Lambeau Field wearing purple, at a similar time of year, three years after his Achilles injury, and leave with a win like this one.

Why it matters: The victory pulls Minnesota into a three-way tie at 7-2 atop the NFC North with Detroit and Chicago, and it leaves the Vikings holding the No. 1 wild-card spot in the NFC playoff picture as of Sunday night. More importantly, it answers the question that had hung over the team during its two-game skid: with Cousins back under center, the offense finally has a steady hand, and the defense showed it can dominate a division rival on the road. The test gets no easier, with a trip to San Francisco next Sunday night, while Green Bay, now 6-4 and clinging to the final wild-card spot, heads to Los Angeles to face the Rams on Thanksgiving Eve.
