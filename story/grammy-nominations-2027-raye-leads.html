Raye Leads 2027 Grammy Nominations With 11 as Taylor Swift Earns Five

Raye leads the field for the 69th Grammy Awards with 11 nominations, ahead of Rosalía with nine, as Taylor Swift breaks Frank Sinatra's album of the year record and the nominations are announced live on Monday morning.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 16, 2026 · 5 min readFacebookX

The nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards were announced on Monday, November 16, and British soul-pop star Raye sits on top of the field with 11 nominations, the most of any artist this year. The Recording Academy revealed the nominees across all 100 categories on Monday morning. The ceremony takes place on Sunday, February 7, 2027, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, airing live on ABC for the first time since 1972 and streaming on Disney+ and Hulu.

Raye leads the nominations with 11 nods, including album of the year for This Music May Contain Hope and both record of the year and song of the year for her breakthrough hit Where Is My Husband! Behind her is Latin star Rosalía with nine nominations, followed by a four-way tie at seven apiece for Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers. Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas and last year's best new artist winner Olivia Dean each picked up six nominations, while Taylor Swift, Geese, Kehlani, Jesse Welles, Jill Scott and Lainey Wilson each earned five.

The album of the year field is eight albums deep: Lost Weekend by Phoebe Bridgers, The Art of Loving by Olivia Dean, Dandelion by Ella Langley, The Romantic by Bruno Mars, This Music May Contain Hope by Raye, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love by Olivia Rodrigo, Lux (Complete Works) by Rosalía and The Life of a Showgirl by Taylor Swift.

Record of the year, which honours the performance and production of a single track, features Man I Need by Olivia Dean, Choosin' Texas by Ella Langley, I Just Might by Bruno Mars, Where Is My Husband! by Raye, the cure by Olivia Rodrigo, Berghain by Rosalía with Björk and Yves Tumor, The Fate of Ophelia by Taylor Swift, and Dracula by Tame Impala with Jennie.

Song of the year, the songwriters' prize, goes to Berghain by Rosalía, Björk and Yves Tumor; Choosin' Texas by Ella Langley; the cure by Olivia Rodrigo; hate that i made you love me by Ariana Grande; I Knew It, I Knew You (From Toy Story 5) by Taylor Swift; Man I Need by Olivia Dean; Risk It All by Bruno Mars; and Where Is My Husband! by Raye. The best new artist category names Angine de Poitrine, Geese, Ella Langley, Slayyyter, Sienna Spiro, Don Toliver, Remi Wolf and The Womack Sisters, with Langley pulling off the rare double of album of the year and best new artist nominations in the same year.

Taylor Swift's five nominations come with a piece of Grammy history attached. Her album of the year nod for The Life of a Showgirl breaks Frank Sinatra's record for the most nominations in the category strictly for solo albums, giving her eight to Sinatra's seven solo album nominations plus one duet album nod. She is also the first female artist to earn five consecutive album of the year nominations for newly released studio albums. She is also up for record of the year with The Fate of Ophelia and song of the year with I Knew It, I Knew You.

Olivia Dean's year is almost as remarkable. The singer, who won best new artist at last year's Grammys, collected six nominations this time, including album of the year for The Art of Loving and record and song of the year for Man I Need. Ella Langley's rise is the country story of the year: her hit Choosin' Texas earned her record and song of the year nominations, her album Dandelion is up for album of the year, and she is also a best new artist contender.

The nominations were not without notable absences. Harry Styles, Justin Bieber, Ed Sheeran, Doja Cat, Hilary Duff and Lizzo were all shut out of this year's nominations, despite releasing music inside the eligibility window. That window covered recordings released between August 31, 2025, and August 28, 2026, and the nominees were chosen by the Recording Academy's nearly 16,000 voting members across 100 categories.

What happens next is a two-month wait. Final-round voting, in which Recording Academy voting members choose the winners, runs from December 10, 2026, to January 7, 2027. Most of the 100 awards will be handed out at the Premiere Ceremony, streaming on Disney+ at 2:30 p.m. ET on February 7, before the main telecast at 7 p.m. ET on ABC. With Raye leading the count and Swift chasing history, February shapes up as a contest between a breakout star's coronation and a veteran's record-breaking run. See the full list of nominees.

Why it matters: These nominations matter because they set the terms of the music industry's biggest night and reveal where its power now sits. Raye's 11 nominations crown a breakout artist rather than an established superstar, while the dominance of women across the big three categories, album, record and song of the year, continues a shift that has defined recent Grammys. Swift's record-breaking eighth solo album of the year nomination cements her as the most consistently recognised album artist in Grammy history, and the ceremony's move to ABC after more than half a century on CBS makes February 7 a test of whether the show can grow its audience on a new network. The two-month voting window means the campaigns, and the arguments about who was snubbed, start now.
