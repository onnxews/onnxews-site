TREASURE Takes the Daesang and Four Awards at the 2026 Korea Grand Music Awards

The YG Entertainment group won the Grand Song Daesang plus Best Artist, Best Hip-Hop and Trend of the Year at the ceremony in Seoul's Gocheok Sky Dome.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 8, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

K-pop boy group TREASURE has achieved another major milestone, winning four awards including a Daesang, or Grand Prize, at the 2026 Korea Grand Music Awards. The group attended the ceremony on November 7 at the Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul, where it received the prestigious 2026 Grand Song Daesang.

Alongside the grand prize, TREASURE took home three additional awards: Best Artist, Best Hip-Hop, and Trend of the Year for a group, bringing its total for the night to four trophies. It was one of the biggest hauls of the evening and a clear statement about the group's standing in the current K-pop landscape.

The achievement highlighted the success of TREASURE's fourth mini album, NEW WAV, and its title track IF I, which were released in June. The album reached No. 1 on iTunes charts in 13 regions and topped four major album charts across South Korea and Japan, giving the group its strongest commercial showing to date.

The album was built around a full hip-hop concept, developed under the production leadership of YG Entertainment executive producer Yang Hyun Suk. The sound marked a confident direction for a group that has spent its career refining its identity, and the Daesang suggests the gamble paid off with both fans and the industry.

A Daesang is the highest honor at a Korean music awards ceremony, and winning one places a group in rare company. The Korea Grand Music Awards, known as the KGMA, is one of the newer major ceremonies on the Korean calendar, but its grand prizes carry real weight, and TREASURE's win puts the group alongside the biggest names in K-pop.

The group's fans, known as TREASURE MAKER, played their part in the night's success. Fan voting and streaming numbers feed into the awards, and the group's ability to mobilize its supporters across Korea, Japan and Southeast Asia has become one of its defining strengths. The four trophies are as much a reward for that organized fandom as for the music itself.

TREASURE debuted under YG Entertainment, one of the big three Korean agencies, and has built its career steadily rather than exploding overnight. The group has toured extensively, released music in both Korean and Japanese, and grown its audience market by market. The NEW WAV era appears to be the moment that steady growth turned into something bigger.

The hip-hop direction is worth noting. K-pop groups often shift concepts between comebacks, but committing a full mini album to hip-hop, with the production oversight of Yang Hyun Suk himself, was a deliberate choice to sharpen the group's image. The Best Hip-Hop award at the KGMA is recognition that the concept landed, not just with fans but with the judges.

The ceremony at the Gocheok Sky Dome, South Korea's largest indoor concert venue, drew a full house, and the night's winners will now carry the momentum into the year-end awards season. For TREASURE, the question is whether the Daesang becomes a turning point, the moment a solidly successful group becomes one of the defining acts of its generation.

For now, the group can celebrate. Four trophies, a grand prize, a No. 1 album in 13 regions and a chart-topping run in Korea and Japan add up to the best year of TREASURE's career so far. The fans who filled the Gocheok Sky Dome on Saturday night will expect the group to build on it, and the industry will be watching to see what comes next.
