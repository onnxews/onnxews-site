RIP TO THE VOICE OF OUR CHILDHOOD: Eric Bauza, Voice of Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, Dies at 46

The Emmy-winning voice of Bugs Bunny, Daffy Duck and so many Looney Tunes favorites has died at 46. Warner Bros. called him “a unique voice talent who brought joy and laughter to millions.”

By Peter Osibajo · Orbit News NetworkOct 26, 2026 · 3 min readShare on FacebookShare on X

Eric Bauza, the Emmy-winning voice of Bugs Bunny, Daffy Duck and so many Looney Tunes favorites, has died at 46.

His family announced his sudden death Sunday. Warner Bros. called him “a unique voice talent who brought joy and laughter to millions.”

That's all, folks — thank you, Eric.
