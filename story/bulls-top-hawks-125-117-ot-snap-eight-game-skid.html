Bulls Snap Eight-Game Skid With 125-117 Overtime Win Over Hawks

Powell scored 33 in his return and Josh Giddey posted a triple-double as Chicago ended an eight-game losing streak in overtime.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 14, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Norman Powell returned to the Chicago Bulls lineup on Friday night and delivered exactly what his team needed, scoring 33 points in a 125-117 overtime win over the Atlanta Hawks.

Powell, in his first game back after missing the past two, scored 15 points in the fourth quarter and overtime combined, and his late scoring carried Chicago to its first home win of the season.

Josh Giddey recorded a triple-double with 15 points, 14 assists and 10 rebounds. His pass to Matas Buzelis for a three-pointer with 23.5 seconds left in overtime gave the Bulls the breathing room they needed to close out the game.

Jalen Johnson did everything he could for Atlanta, finishing with a triple-double of his own: 27 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists. Six Hawks scored in double figures, with Onyeka Okongwu adding 17 points and Nickeil Alexander-Walker 16. Jalen Smith added 15 points and 12 rebounds before leaving the game with an apparent left-hand injury early in overtime, and Isaac Okoro scored 15 while Patrick Williams added 13.

CJ McCollum led the Hawks' backcourt with 20 points, but Atlanta could not hold off Chicago in the extra period. The Bulls opened the fourth quarter with a 10-2 run to take a 94-85 lead before Atlanta battled back. The Bulls outscored the Hawks 15-7 in overtime, turning a game that had slipped away into a win that ended their skid.

Chicago had led by 18 points early in the second quarter, 46-28, and held a 60-53 lead at halftime. Atlanta fought back to trail just 84-83 after three quarters, then tied the game at 110-110 with 1:08 left in regulation to force overtime.

The win snapped an eight-game losing streak for the Bulls, who improved to 2-9. It was their first victory at the United Center this season, and the relief in the building was obvious as the final buzzer sounded.

Atlanta fell to 7-6 and has now lost two straight games. Both teams are 1-1 in East Group C of the NBA Cup, and the Hawks will be frustrated to have let a game they trailed by 18 slip through their fingers in overtime.

The overtime period belonged to Chicago's backcourt. Powell's late scoring was the difference, but Giddey's composure was just as important. His triple-double showed his all-around value, and his pass to Buzelis for the late three was the kind of play that wins close games.

For the Bulls, the win was about more than the standings. An eight-game losing streak can break a locker room, especially for a young team, and the first home win of the season gives the players something to build on. The road trip to Minnesota will be a test of whether the win was a turning point or just a good night.

Chicago was without rookie Caleb Wilson, who fractured his right hand and underwent surgery this week. The Bulls now travel to Minnesota to face the Timberwolves on Saturday, November 14, at 8 p.m. Eastern, while Atlanta visits Milwaukee on Sunday, November 15. Read the report from Reuters

Why it matters: The win will not change Chicago's season on its own, the Bulls are still 2-9, but it matters because it ended an eight-game skid and gave a young team its first home win in front of its own fans. Powell's return showed how much the Bulls missed his scoring, and Giddey's triple-double was a reminder of what the backcourt can be. For Atlanta, blowing an 18-point comeback in overtime is the kind of loss that lingers, and two straight defeats have cooled a promising start.
