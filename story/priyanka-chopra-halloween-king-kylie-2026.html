PRIYANKA CHOPRA CHANNELS 'KING KYLIE' FOR HALLOWEEN WITH NICK JONAS AND MALTI MARIE

Priyanka Chopra Jonas channelled 'King Kylie' for Halloween alongside Nick Jonas and their daughter Malti Marie, Bollywood Hungama reported on November 1.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 1, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Priyanka Chopra Jonas channelled 'King Kylie' for Halloween with Nick Jonas and Malti Marie, Bollywood Hungama reported on November 1, sharing a look at the family's costumes.

The Halloween looks landed in a busy weekend of celebrity costume reveals, with Janhvi Kapoor recreating her mother Sridevi's iconic Nagina look and Shah Rukh Khan stepping out for a drive to capture a King-themed display ahead of his birthday.

Priyanka and Nick have made a habit of coordinated family costumes, and this year's 'King Kylie' turn — a nod to Kylie Jenner's internet-era alter ego — kept the tradition going with their daughter in the picture.

Bollywood Hungama's November 1 features roundup also carried news from across the industry, including Shah Rukh Khan's King distribution move and new music from Himesh Reshammiya.

Halloween costume season has become one of the biggest celebrity content moments of the year, and the Chopra Jonas family's entry was among the most shared of the weekend.

Reporting: Bollywood Hungama
