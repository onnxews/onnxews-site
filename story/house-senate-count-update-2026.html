House Count Widens to 251-153 for Democrats as Two Senate Races Stay Uncalled

Decision Desk HQ's latest tally puts Democrats further past the House majority line. In Alaska and Mississippi, both Democratic challengers lead by fewer than 2,000 votes.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Decision Desk HQ has called 251 House seats for Democrats and 153 for Republicans, with 31 still uncalled, in its update on the morning of November 5. The Senate stands at 54 seats for the Democratic caucus and 44 for Republicans, with Alaska and Mississippi still uncalled.

The Associated Press, which calls races on a more conservative standard, had called 235 House seats for Democrats and 146 for Republicans, with Democrats leading in 26 more districts and Republicans leading in 28.

In Mississippi, Decision Desk HQ reports Scott Colom on 466,464 votes and Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith on 464,612, a Colom lead of 1,852 votes. In Alaska, Mary Peltola leads Senator Dan Sullivan by 637 first-choice votes, 102,962 to 102,325. Neither lead is a race call.

The Alaska race may not be called for more than a week because of ranked-choice tabulation, Reuters reported, while the timeline for a decision in Mississippi remained unclear. If Democrats won both, their Senate majority would grow to 56-44.

NBC News projects Democrats gaining 28 House seats for a total of 258, compared with 177 for Republicans, though other outlets are more cautious and dozens of House races remain uncalled. Neither chamber's majority is in doubt, but final seat counts may shift until counting and certification finish.

Reporting: Decision Desk HQ
