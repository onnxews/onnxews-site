Bears-Seahawks Monday Night Football Draws 16.4 Million Viewers, Biggest in Years

The 34-17 Bears win over the Seahawks drew 16.4 million viewers, the most for an ESPN-only Monday Night Football game since November 2019.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

The Chicago Bears made a statement with a 34-17 win over the defending Super Bowl champion Seattle Seahawks on Monday Night Football, where a massive audience got to witness quarterback Caleb Williams, defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons and company orchestrate an impressive victory.

The Bears-Seahawks Week 8 game drew 16.4 million viewers, which was the most-watched ESPN-only Monday Night Football game dating back to November 2019, a span of 76 games airing on ESPN. It is also the third most-watched Week 8 game of the ESPN era, which began in 2006.

The Bears offense out-dueled the league's best defense in the Seahawks, with Williams becoming the first quarterback to pass for 200 yards against Seattle this season, throwing for 247, while Chicago became the first team to score three rushing touchdowns, all from D'Andre Swift, and scored the most points, 34, against the Seahawks this season.

Many analysts noted this game could be an NFC Championship preview featuring two of the NFC's best teams and a slew of intriguing matchups featuring best versus best.

Chicago has been featured on back-to-back prime-time games where they have drawn a massive audience, including Week 7 against the New England Patriots. Now, the Bears will host the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Sunday Night Football in the final of three straight prime-time games before their bye week.
