Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Orbit News Network (onnxews.com) is an independent news site published by Peter Osibajo. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Information we collect

Orbit News Network does not run its own analytics, does not offer user accounts, and does not ask you for your name, email address, or any other personal information to read the news. The site's newsletter signup is not connected to any mailing service, so no address you enter there is collected or stored.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare Pages, may collect standard server logs when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. Those logs are used for security and for keeping the site running, and we do not use them to identify individual readers.

Cookies and advertising

The site stores one preference in your own browser — your dark or light theme choice — using local storage. It does not set tracking cookies of its own.

Orbit News Network uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

Links to other sites

Articles link to outside sources, and the site links to its Facebook, X, and Instagram pages. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Children's privacy

This is a general-audience news site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach Orbit News Network through the channels on our Contact page.
