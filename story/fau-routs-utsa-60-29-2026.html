Florida Atlantic Routs UTSA 60-29, Snapping the Roadrunners' Five-Game Win Streak

The Owls piled up 563 yards, including 339 on the ground, as Caden Veltkamp threw four touchdown passes in a Thursday night stunner in Boca Raton.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Florida Atlantic snapped UTSA's five-game winning streak with a 60-29 rout in American Conference action Thursday night at Flagler Credit Union Stadium in Boca Raton.

The Owls totaled 563 yards, the most by a UTSA opponent this season, including 339 rushing yards. Quarterback Caden Veltkamp threw for 210 yards and four touchdowns on 17-of-25 passing, and Easton Messer caught seven passes for 85 yards and three scores — and threw a touchdown pass of his own.

Leonard Farrow led the ground game with 126 yards on 12 rushes, while Ethan Ervin added 92 yards. Florida Atlantic led 35-22 at halftime and pulled away in the second half, scoring on a fumble recovery deep in UTSA territory to open the third quarter.

UTSA quarterback Owen McCown completed 20 of 35 passes for 265 yards and three touchdowns, and Mekhi Anderson registered his first career 100-yard receiving game with six catches for 117 yards and two touchdowns. Michael Petro matched the program record with a 54-yard field goal as time expired in the first half.

The Roadrunners fell to 7-2 overall and 4-1 in the league with their first loss since September 19, while the Owls improved to 6-3 and 3-2. The result shakes up the American Conference title race and UTSA's hopes of a playoff spot from outside the power conferences.
