Democrats Vow to Rein In Trump and Focus on Prices as He Shows Off White House Ballroom

A day after sweeping Congress, Democrats promised affordability and accountability. The president spent his first post-election appearance touring his under-construction ballroom.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Democrats vowed on Wednesday to use their sweeping midterm victory to rein in President Donald Trump, setting up two years of likely deadlock in Washington, Reuters reported.

House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries said the incoming majority would be focused on affordability and accountability. Democrats campaigned on the cost of living, on ending the war with Iran that has driven up fuel prices, and on halting what they describe as corruption benefiting billionaires.

Trump spent his first post-election appearance showing off his under-construction White House ballroom, moving on to non-electoral matters a day after his party's stunning losses. He has said he will work with Democrats and that he accepts the results.

The scale of the Democratic win was broad: the party flipped the Senate to a majority of at least 54-44 and was projected to gain at least 30 seats in the House once counting finished, with two Senate races in Alaska and Mississippi still uncalled, leaving open the possibility the Senate margin could grow to 56-44.

Republicans lost ground in 99 percent of the roughly 2,100 counties where most votes had been counted as of Wednesday morning, according to tallies compiled by Reuters and USA Today. It will be the first time Trump has had to contend with Democrats controlling both chambers of Congress.

Reporting: Reuters
