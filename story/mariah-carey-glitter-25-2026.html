JUSTICE FOR GLITTER: Mariah Carey Opens the Vault for 25th Anniversary

Mariah Carey marked 25 years of Glitter with a 27-track deluxe edition packed with vault recordings, remixes, and live tracks.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 30, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Mariah Carey released an expanded edition of Glitter on October 30, 2026, marking the 25th anniversary of the album that also served as the soundtrack to her film of the same name.

The digital deluxe edition runs to 27 tracks, adding 15 recordings to the original album, including material from Carey's vault, new remixes, and live performances.

Among the previously unreleased selections are an early version of “Lead The Way,” a Mariah-only version of “Want You,” an alternate vocal take of “Out Here On My Own,” and an a cappella version of “All My Life.”

The set also traces the development of “Never Too Far” through a writing-session interlude and rough demo, and includes a live “Glitter Medley” recorded on the Caution Tour.

New interpretations include a Channel Tres remix of “All My Life” and a reworked version of “Didn't Mean To Turn You On” featuring Rochelle Jordan, alongside remixes of “Loverboy.” The anniversary release also marks Glitter's first vinyl release since 2001, with a standard Midnight Purple 2LP and a limited Glittering Pink 2LP, plus a restored 4K version of the “Loverboy” video.
