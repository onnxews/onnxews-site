Kohli Passes Tendulkar as Leading ODI Chase Scorer in India Win Over New Zealand

Kohli's 74 off 51 balls powered India to a five-wicket win in Auckland and took him past Tendulkar's 8,720 runs in ODI chases, in 73 fewer innings.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Virat Kohli delivered a masterclass in controlled batting during India's opening ODI against New Zealand in Auckland on Wednesday, steering his side to a comfortable five-wicket victory under the DLS method.

Chasing a revised target of 143 runs in 32 overs, India got there in 22.4 overs, securing a 1-0 lead in the five-match series. Kohli was the standout performer, anchoring the chase with a fluent 74 runs from just 51 deliveries, with 13 fours and a six.

The innings saw Kohli reach a significant career milestone, as he surpassed Sachin Tendulkar to become the leading run-scorer in ODI run chases. Tendulkar amassed 8,720 runs in 242 innings; Kohli has now moved to 8,741 runs in just 169 innings, at an average of 66.21 and a strike rate of 95.03.

Kohli also equalled Ricky Ponting's record for the most runs scored against New Zealand in ODI cricket, with both players now on 1,971 runs, and became the first batter to score 13,000 ODI runs batting at No. 3. It was his 80th ODI fifty, and his 113-run stand with Shubman Gill, who made 37, put India firmly on course.

New Zealand had been bowled out for 185 in 40.2 overs, with Arshdeep Singh taking 4-70, Gurnoor Brar 3-44 and Anshul Kamboj 2-22. The second ODI will be played on Saturday at Wellington. Kohli reached the Tendulkar record on the eve of his 38th birthday.
