Vance Blames Iran War for Republican Midterm Defeat in Break With Trump

"The reason we lost is the war," the vice president wrote, going further than the president in blaming the conflict and sketching a 2028 platform of ending it.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Vice President JD Vance blamed the war with Iran for the Republican Party's historic midterm losses on Wednesday, breaking his silence hours after Democrats swept control of Congress.

"The reason we lost is the war. The president himself said so last night," Vance wrote in a lengthy post on X. "If you'd like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it, then do so. Argue it was a worthwhile cost to pay. Agree or disagree, that's at least honest."

Vance went further than President Donald Trump, who had called the war only a piece of the outcome. The vice president cited a pollster who he said listed four reasons voters who backed Trump in 2024 abandoned Republicans in the midterms: ending the war, focusing on the cost of living, putting America first, and changing the U.S. relationship with Israel.

An NBC News exit poll found 74 percent of voters said the war with Iran was a crucial or major factor in their vote, while 37 percent said the United States made the right decision to take military action. An October Pew Research Center poll found 65 percent disapproved of the way Trump has handled the conflict.

The post was widely read as Vance distancing himself from Trump's foreign policy ahead of a possible 2028 White House bid, sketching a platform centered on ending the war, tackling affordability and refocusing on domestic challenges. CNN reported that Florida Governor Ron DeSantis is also making quick moves as the 2028 race begins, and that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth could face an impeachment vote in the House as soon as next week.
