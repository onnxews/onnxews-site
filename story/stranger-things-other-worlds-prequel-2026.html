Netflix Orders Stranger Things Prequel 'Other Worlds,' Set in 1955 Nevada

The prequel will be set in Marion, Nevada, in 1955, years before the original series, and will explore the events that created Henry Creel, the villain who became Vecna.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Netflix has ordered a live-action prequel to Stranger Things, sending the franchise back to 1955 and a small Nevada town decades before the events of the original series. The new show, titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, is scheduled to premiere in 2028.

The announcement arrived on Friday, November 6, as part of Stranger Things Day, the annual celebration marking the fictional date Will Byers disappeared in the first season, November 6, 1983. Netflix released a teaser that opens on the words April 19, 1955, Marion, Nevada, and closes with the tagline Before Hawkins, there was Marion.

The series will explore an earlier chapter of the show's mythology, including the events that led to Henry Creel's exposure to the otherworldly substance that gave him his powers and eventually transformed him into Vecna, the villain of the original series. It takes place before both the original show and the stage production Stranger Things: The First Shadow, which is set in 1959 and follows a teenage Henry Creel.

Matt and Ross Duffer are co-creating the series with Kate Trefry, a longtime Stranger Things writer who wrote the stage play The First Shadow. Trefry will serve as showrunner, writer and executive producer. The Duffers will executive produce through their Upside Down Pictures banner, alongside Shawn Levy of 21 Laps and other producers.

BAFTA and Golden Globe-winning director Joe Wright, known for Atonement, Pride and Prejudice and Darkest Hour, will direct the first three episodes and serve as an executive producer. Production is planned for New Mexico, with filming reportedly set to begin in 2027. Netflix has not announced a cast, and the series will introduce an entirely new set of characters.

The project is an exception within the Duffers' new overall deal with Paramount, which followed the expiration of their Netflix agreement in April 2026. The brothers remain involved with the franchise they created, even as their new work moves to a different studio, and Netflix is keeping the prequel in-house as part of the Stranger Things universe.

The announcement drew immediate attention from fans, who have been reading the teaser's imagery closely. The teaser shows a disturbance in the night sky over the Nevada desert that viewers have taken for a UFO, and some outlets have noted that the 1950s setting and the Duffers' hints suggest a story with an alien or outer-space thread running through it.

The original series ended in late 2025, and Other Worlds joins a growing list of follow-ups, including the animated Stranger Things: Tales from '85 and the stage show, as the franchise's fourth project. The final season left the door open for more stories in the same world, and the prequel is the most substantial live-action continuation Netflix has announced so far.

For Netflix, the timing is deliberate. Stranger Things remains one of the streamer's biggest global hits, and a prequel set in the Nevada desert, decades before Hawkins, gives the company a new story to tell without recasting the original characters or revisiting the ending fans have already seen. Whether it can capture the same audience without them is the question the next two years of production will answer.
