HOOSIER HONOR: IU's Markus Burton Lands on Bob Cousy Award Watch List

The 2023 Indiana Mr. Basketball is one of 20 players on the watch list for the award given to the top point guard in college basketball.

By Peter Osibajo · Orbit News Network · Oct 27, 2026 · 2 min read

BLOOMINGTON — IU guard Markus Burton is officially on the national radar. The Hoosier is one of 20 players named to the watch list for the 2027 Bob Cousy Award, given annually to the top point guard in college basketball.

Burton's path home is the story. The 2023 Indiana Mr. Basketball starred at Penn High School, then spent three seasons at Notre Dame, where he averaged 19.1 points per game and became one of the ACC's most reliable scorers.

Then he came home. Burton transferred to Indiana, giving the Hoosiers a proven, high-scoring lead guard — and giving Bloomington a hometown star to build around.

The Cousy Award, named for Hall of Fame Boston Celtics point guard Bob Cousy, goes to the best floor general in the country. Making the 20-man watch list puts Burton in the conversation; the list will be narrowed as the season goes on, with the winner announced around the Final Four.

The season starts fast: IU opens Monday, Nov. 2 against Eastern Illinois at Assembly Hall, where Burton will get his first chance to show the home crowd what the numbers already say.

For a program with Indiana's guard tradition, a Cousy watch list nod is more than a preseason honor — it is a sign the Hoosiers might have the piece every contender needs: a point guard who can take over a game.

Burton's Notre Dame numbers tell the story of a scorer who got better every year. He arrived in South Bend as a freshman with a scorer's reputation and left as the kind of guard opposing coaches game-planned around — the rare player who can create his own shot late in the clock.

For IU, the transfer is part of a bigger bet: that proven college production beats potential. Burton, a senior guard with three years of ACC scoring on his résumé, is the centerpiece of that approach.
