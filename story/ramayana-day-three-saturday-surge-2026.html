Ramayana Roars Back on Saturday With a Huge Jump at the Box Office

After a Friday dip, the Ranbir Kapoor and Yash epic collected nearly Rs 24 crore by noon on Saturday, putting a Rs 70 crore day within reach.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Ramayana: Part I has roared back at the box office on its third day. After a dip on Friday, the mythological epic starring Ranbir Kapoor and Yash witnessed a strong turnaround on Saturday, November 7, collecting Rs 23.95 crore net across all language versions by 12 noon, according to Bollywood Hungama, and raising hopes of a massive Saturday total.

The morning numbers point to a very big day. With Rs 23.95 crore already banked by midday, the film has a realistic opportunity to cross the Rs 70 crore mark across all languages on Saturday, provided the momentum strengthens through the evening and night shows. A Rs 70 crore Saturday would represent growth of about 52.71 percent over Friday's Rs 45.84 crore.

It would also top the opening day. Ramayana opened on Thursday with Rs 62.47 crore net, before declining on Friday, so a Rs 70 crore Saturday would beat the opening-day figure by nearly 12 percent. The three-day domestic net total would reach Rs 178.31 crore, putting the film within striking distance of the coveted Rs 200 crore milestone on Sunday.

The advance booking told the same story before a single Saturday ticket was torn. The film sold 12,19,924 tickets worth Rs 47.05 crore gross across 22,572 shows for its third day, with Maharashtra leading the charge at Rs 9.81 crore and Karnataka close behind at Rs 8.59 crore. Morning occupancy stood at 28.9 percent and improved to 35.1 percent for the afternoon shows.

The regional breakdown underlines how wide the film's appeal runs. Maharashtra, Karnataka and the National Capital Region have emerged as the three strongest markets on Saturday, and the film is playing across multiple language versions, a strategy that has become standard for India's biggest releases but still depends on the film delivering in every market.

The turnaround matters because Friday's dip had raised questions. After the record-breaking advance booking and a huge opening day, a slide to Rs 45.84 crore on Friday looked like the film might be front-loaded, with fans rushing in on day one and fading fast. Saturday's surge suggests the opposite: word of mouth is bringing audiences back, and families are turning out for the weekend.

Ramayana arrived in theaters on Thursday, November 5, a day ahead of the Diwali weekend's other big releases, and it has led the pack from the start. Director Nitesh Tiwari's epic, with Kapoor and Yash headlining one of the most anticipated Indian films in years, set a pre-Diwali advance-booking record before release, and the opening-day numbers confirmed the hype.

The competition is not standing still. Suriya's Scene opened strongly on Friday with Rs 34.25 crore worldwide, and the weekend will test whether both films can thrive side by side or whether Ramayana's Saturday surge squeezes its rivals. For now, the epic is the story of the weekend.

If Saturday finishes near Rs 70 crore, Ramayana will head into Sunday with the Rs 200 crore milestone in sight and the Diwali box office firmly in its grip. The question is no longer whether the film is a hit, but how big the hit will be.
