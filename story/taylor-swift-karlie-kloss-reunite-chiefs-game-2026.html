Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss Reunite at Chiefs Game, Nearly a Decade After Their Last Photo Together

The onetime best friends were photographed together for the first time since 2018, sitting side by side in Swift's Arrowhead suite as the Chiefs beat the Jets 35-12 on Travis Kelce's 200th-game touchdown.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 9, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss were photographed together for the first time in nearly a decade on Sunday, sitting side by side in Swift's private suite at Arrowhead Stadium as the Kansas City Chiefs beat the New York Jets 35-12. The reunion of the onetime best friends set social media alight and instantly became the most talked-about celebrity moment of the weekend.

Swift invited Kloss to join her inside her suite to cheer on her husband, Travis Kelce, and the Chiefs. The two sat shoulder to shoulder, smiling and laughing through the game, surrounded by Swift's mother Andrea, her father Scott, and her mother-in-law Donna Kelce. Photographs and video from the suite spread across social media within minutes of the pair being spotted together.

The last time the two were photographed together was August 2018, when Kloss went backstage at the Reputation Stadium Tour's stop in Nashville. In the years since, their friendship has been the subject of endless speculation, with fans and gossip columns alike treating a rumored rift as fact — even though neither woman has ever publicly confirmed a falling out.

The pair became close around the 2013 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, and Kloss famously appeared in Swift's 2015 video for 'Bad Blood.' Their friendship was one of the defining celebrity pairings of the mid-2010s, documented in magazine covers, Instagram posts and joint appearances, before the public sightings quietly stopped. Kloss attended Swift's Eras Tour concert in August 2023 and, according to reports, her wedding to Kelce in July 2026.

Swift's game-day look drew almost as much attention as her guest. The 36-year-old singer wore a red-and-pink striped Tommy Hilfiger sweater polo, a nod to the Chiefs' colors, and a custom Stephanie Gottlieb necklace featuring Kelce's jersey number, 87. Page Six reported that the cable-knit sweater was on sale for $56 at kickoff and sold out within minutes of Swift appearing on television.

The choice of designer was no accident. Kelce announced his partnership with Tommy Hilfiger in March 2026, becoming a global ambassador for the fashion brand, and Swift has made a habit of working his endorsements and the team's colors into her game-day outfits this season. She has been a frequent fixture at Chiefs home games since she began dating Kelce in 2023.

There was plenty to celebrate on the field as well. Kelce scored in the third quarter, taking a two-handed shovel toss from quarterback Patrick Mahomes on fourth-and-1 near the goal line and carrying it in for a 1-yard touchdown that stretched Kansas City's lead to 21-12. The touchdown came in Kelce's 200th career NFL game, a milestone for one of the greatest tight ends in league history.

The reunion also comes during a busy stretch for Swift. She was recently inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame, and she has been making appearances in support of her original song for Toy Story 5 as awards season approaches. Reports have also suggested she is working on new music, with one outlet claiming she has enough material for her next two albums.

Whether the Arrowhead appearance marks a genuine rekindling or simply a friendly invitation to a football game, the reaction online suggested fans had been waiting for it. Within hours, the photographs were being shared as evidence that one of pop culture's longest-running cold wars was over. For two women who have spent a decade being asked about each other, a single afternoon in a stadium suite said more than any statement could.
