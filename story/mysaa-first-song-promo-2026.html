MYSAA DROPS PROMO FOR FIRST SONG 'O MYSAA RE' — FULL TRACK OUT NOVEMBER 2

The makers of Mysaa, starring Rashmika Mandanna, have unveiled a promo for the film's first single, 'O Mysaa Re', with the track scheduled for release on November 2.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 1, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

The makers of Mysaa, starring Rashmika Mandanna, have unveiled a new promo for the film's first single, 'O Mysaa Re'. The track is scheduled to be released on November 2, with the announcement video offering a brief glimpse of its visual treatment and music.

The film is produced by Unformula Films and directed by Rawindra Pulle, and is set to arrive in cinemas on November 27, 2026.

The newly released promo features a fiery, silhouette-driven visual, hinting at the song's intense tone. Composed by Jakes Bejoy, the track is expected to reflect the earthy setting and action-oriented world of the film.

The upcoming single will be the first musical offering from the film's album and is expected to provide audiences with a closer look at the film's soundscape.

With 'O Mysaa Re' set to release on November 2, the makers are beginning the film's music campaign ahead of its theatrical debut later in the month.
