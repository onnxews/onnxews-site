Russell Brand Found Guilty of Rape and Sexual Assault by London Jury

The 51-year-old comedian was convicted on four of seven charges after a trial at Southwark Crown Court and was remanded in custody ahead of sentencing on Monday.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

British comedian Russell Brand was found guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault by a jury at London's Southwark Crown Court on Thursday. He was acquitted of two other rape charges and one count of attempted indecent assault.

The charges involved six women, with alleged offences spanning from 1999 to 2009 in locations including a hotel room, a London bar, a radio station, a recording studio and Brand's own home. The rape conviction relates to an assault on a 21-year-old fan Brand met after a performance at the Hammersmith Apollo in 2009. Brand, 51, denied all the charges.

Judge Joel Bennathan refused Brand bail and remanded him in custody, telling him he should expect a significant custodial term. He will be sentenced on Monday, November 9. Rape carries a sentencing range of four to 19 years in custody, and legal experts have estimated Brand could receive around eight or nine years.

The allegations first became public in September 2023, when Channel 4's Dispatches programme and The Sunday Times published a joint investigation. Six complainants gave evidence during the three-week trial, which began on October 12.

After the verdict, London's Metropolitan Police confirmed Brand is under investigation for two further rape allegations made by two other women. The force's commissioner, Sir Mark Rowley, said he would not be surprised if more people came forward.

Reporting: USA Today
