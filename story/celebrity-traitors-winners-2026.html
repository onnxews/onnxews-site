FAITHFULS WIN THE CASTLE: Romesh and Sebastian Take Celebrity Traitors

After weeks of deception at Ardross Castle, two Faithfuls outlasted the Traitors and split £83,000 for charity in a dramatic final.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 30, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Romesh Ranganathan and Sebastian Croft were crowned the winners of The Celebrity Traitors series 2 in the BBC final broadcast on Friday night, October 30, 2026.

The pair played as Faithfuls throughout the game and, after eliminating the last remaining Traitor, King Kenny, chose to end the game and split the £83,000 prize pot between their chosen charities.

Croft said his share would go to Choose Love, an organisation he had previously volunteered for, with a focus on refugees and displaced people, including queer refugees and displaced people.

Ranganathan said his share would go to the Refugee Council, a cause he described as very close to his heart, helping displaced people who need help.

The final had briefly looked set to end with singer James Blunt joining Ranganathan and Croft as a winner, but Blunt was ultimately banished by his fellow remaining Faithfuls. Earlier in the finale, Traitors King Kenny and Maya Jama saw their game unravel after their decision to target actor Michael Sheen raised suspicions, while Leigh-Anne Pinnock was also banished at the final round table.
