Musk Spent Nearly $500 Million on the Midterms. It Bought Him Almost Nothing.

The world's richest man was the cycle's biggest individual donor by far, but most of his 106 backed candidates lost, including in Texas, where he spent more than $200 million.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Elon Musk put about $474 million into the 2026 midterms, according to a New York Times tally of Federal Election Commission and IRS filings through October 14, making him the election's single largest individual donor by a vast margin. Other tallies put the figure at nearly $500 million.

Musk's money flowed to Republican House and Senate candidates through his own super PAC, America PAC, at about $216 million, the Senate Leadership Fund at $168 million, and the Congressional Leadership Fund for House races at $30 million.

He backed Republicans in 106 House and Senate races. A Forbes analysis found 44 of those candidates had lost, including in the races that mattered most to him: Ken Paxton's bid for a Texas Senate seat, Greg Abbott's bid to remain Texas governor, and Vivek Ramaswamy's bid for governor of Ohio.

The largest share of Musk's spending, about $212 million, went toward helping Paxton beat Democrat James Talarico for the Texas Senate seat. In all, Musk funneled around $225 million into Texas, according to CNBC, and the state delivered a stunning rebuke, with the Senate seat and the governorship both flipping blue.

America PAC spent about $100 million across 44 separate races, winning three and losing 29, with 12 yet to be called, according to CNBC figures cited by Gizmodo. Musk's companies also took a hit on the stock market on Wednesday, wiping out an estimated $120 billion in value, according to Bloomberg. He is worth about $900 billion, so the midterm spending amounted to roughly one-twentieth of one percent of his wealth.
