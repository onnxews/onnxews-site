Suriya's Scene Storms Past 100 Crore Worldwide in Its Opening Weekend

The makers say Suriya's Tamil action-comedy took more than 145 crore rupees worldwide in three days; independent trackers put it at 116.65 crore. Either way, it is one of the biggest openings of the actor's career.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 9, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Suriya's Scene has crossed a major milestone at the worldwide box office within its opening three days, with the makers announcing on Monday that the film had collected more than 145 crore rupees globally since its theatrical debut on November 6. Zhagaram Studios shared a new box office poster on its official social media handles, announcing the worldwide gross and describing the film as a blockbuster.

Independent trade trackers put the figure somewhat lower, at 116.65 crore rupees worldwide for the first three days, according to Sacnilk figures reported by Cinema Express and Filmfare. On that accounting, the film's India gross stands at 72.90 crore rupees, with domestic net collections of 62.75 crore rupees and an overseas gross of 43.75 crore rupees. The gap between the makers' announcement and the trade estimates is normal for Indian box office reporting, where studio figures and independent trackers routinely differ.

By either measure, the opening is a strong one. The film collected 23.25 crore rupees net in India on Sunday, November 8, a 14.3 percent increase over Saturday's 20.35 crore rupees, showing that word of mouth built through the weekend rather than fading. Pinkvilla reported a global weekend of about 120 crore rupees, with a $5 million overseas weekend, and noted that Scene emerged as the second-biggest weekend opener of Suriya's career after Karuppu, which began with 121 crore rupees globally.

Scene is directed by Jithu Madhavan, making his Tamil directorial debut after the Malayalam hits Romancham and Aavesham. It stars Suriya as police officer Inbaraaj, alongside Nazriya Nazim and Naslen, and it is the actor's 47th film in a lead role. The film was produced by Suriya and Jyothika's 2D Entertainment with Zhagaram Studios, and it combines action and comedy in a cop story that has clearly connected with audiences.

The release was timed for Diwali, the biggest moviegoing period of the year in India, and it arrived in theaters on November 6 across more than 6,000 shows in India. Advance booking had already shown a strong response, and the film posted the highest opening of Suriya's career overseas, beating the $1.45 million of Vishwanath and Sons with a $1.87 million overseas day one.

The opening is Suriya's third big release of the year, and it keeps up a remarkable run. His highest-grossing film remains Karuppu, which took 310 crore rupees worldwide and became the first 300-crore grosser of his career. Scene's opening puts it in a position to challenge that mark if it holds through its second weekend, though its lifetime total will depend on how it performs once the Diwali holiday ends.

For Tamil cinema, the result is another reminder of its commercial strength. The industry's biggest stars now open films at a scale that rivals Bollywood, and the overseas market — where Tamil audiences in Malaysia, Singapore, the Gulf and North America turn out in force — has become a major part of the box office story. Overseas takings accounted for about 37.5 percent of Scene's worldwide gross in its first three days.

The film is still running in theaters, and its final verdict will depend on its lifetime collection. But the opening weekend has already done its job: Scene is a hit, Suriya has another blockbuster on his hands, and the makers' poster says it plainly enough — the film is, in their words, a blockbuster.
