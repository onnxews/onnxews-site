HARRYWEEN IS BACK: STYLES OPENS AS ELVIS, THEN BECOMES HIS 16-YEAR-OLD SELF

Harry Styles brought back Harryween at Madison Square Garden on Friday, opening as Elvis Presley before changing into the outfit from his 2010 X Factor audition.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 31, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Harry Styles performed the first of two 'Harryween' concerts at Madison Square Garden on Friday, October 30, the penultimate date of his 30-night residency at the venue.

He initially appeared dressed as Elvis Presley, wearing the King's signature white jumpsuit with a cape and gold sunglasses, while his backing band and dancers dressed as various versions of Styles from throughout his career.

Later in the show, Styles changed into the outfit he wore for his original 2010 X Factor audition and performed Stevie Wonder's 'Isn't She Lovely,' the song he sang for judges Simon Cowell, Nicole Scherzinger and Louis Walsh at age 16.

Elvis's granddaughter Riley Keough watched from the crowd alongside Styles's reported fiancée Zoë Kravitz, who was dressed as a devil in red with horns.

The second Harryween show follows on Saturday, closing out the Halloween weekend residency.
