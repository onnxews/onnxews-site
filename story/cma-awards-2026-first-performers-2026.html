Ella Langley, Tim McGraw and Miranda Lambert Lead First Performers Announced for 60th CMA Awards

The Country Music Association revealed eight performers for the November 18 show, which will also feature a tribute to Dolly Parton and a first CMA appearance in a decade from Taylor Swift.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

The Country Music Association revealed the first batch of performers for the 60th Annual CMA Awards on Thursday, with Cody Johnson, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Tim McGraw, Kacey Musgraves, The Red Clay Strays, Zach Top and Tucker Wetmore all set to take the stage.

The milestone show will air live from Nashville's Bridgestone Arena on Wednesday, November 18, on ABC and Disney+, streaming the next day on Hulu. Lainey Wilson will return to host for a third consecutive year, her second time leading the ceremony solo.

Ella Langley leads this year's nominees with nine nominations, including Entertainer of the Year and Album of the Year, after taking home three CMA Awards last year. Miranda Lambert follows with seven nominations, bringing her career total to 70, the most nominations ever for a female artist in CMA Awards history.

The ceremony will also feature a special tribute performance for the late Dolly Parton as part of its 60th anniversary celebration. The association said the lineup brings together genre icons and the artists shaping country music's future.

Taylor Swift, nominated for Single of the Year for "I Knew It, I Knew You," revealed she will attend the awards show for the first time in 10 years. More performers and presenters will be announced in the coming weeks.
