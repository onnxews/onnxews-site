If He Splits The Bill On A First Date, He's Broke

A man who asks you out and then asks you to split the bill just told you everything you need to know about him. It's not about the money — it's about pride, effort, and what he can't cover.

POPeter OsibajoOrbit News Network · Nov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Let's call it what it is. A man who asks you out and then asks you to split the bill is broke. Not “going through a rough patch” broke — broke in the head.

You don't invite somebody out and then slide half the check across the table like it's a group project. You asked her to be there. The dinner is on you. That's not tradition, that's basic pride.

And stop pretending it's about equality. It's not. It's about a man who can't cover a $60 dinner trying to make his cheapness sound like feminism. Women aren't gold diggers for expecting the man who invited them out to pay — he's just cheap and too embarrassed to admit it.

If $60 for dinner breaks him, rent will destroy him. A car note will destroy him. A real life with a woman will destroy him. The first date is the audition — if this is his best performance, he's not getting the part.

And if she offers to pay and he actually takes her up on it? He just failed the easiest test in dating. A real man says “I got it” even if his account is crying. Pride costs nothing. Being cheap costs you the girl.

Don't let him gaslight you with “it's 2026.” 2026 didn't make men cheap. Broke men did.

A man who splits the bill on the first date is telling you straight up: I can't provide, I can't lead, and I don't even have the pride to pretend. Believe him the first time.
