Bratt and Caufield Deliver Overtime Winners as Devils and Canadiens Survive Late Collapses

Jesper Bratt scored 1:26 into overtime after New Jersey blew a 4-1 lead, and Cole Caufield needed just 30 seconds to beat Utah, in an 11-game NHL night.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Jesper Bratt scored a breakaway goal 1:26 into overtime to send the New Jersey Devils to a 5-4 victory over the visiting St. Louis Blues on Thursday night, rescuing a win after the Devils blew a 4-1 lead late in the third period.

Anthony Mantha had two goals and an assist, and Luke Hughes added a goal and an assist for New Jersey, which has won three straight and seven of its past eight games. The Blues tied it with three goals in the third period from Mason McTavish, Philip Broberg and Dalibor Dvorsky before Bratt beat Jordan Binnington on the breakaway.

In Montreal, Cole Caufield scored 30 seconds into overtime as the Canadiens defeated the visiting Utah Mammoth 5-4. Captain Nick Suzuki had a goal and two assists after missing the past six games with a hand injury, and Montreal is now on a nine-game point streak.

Logan Cooley recorded his second hat trick in a week and added an assist for Utah, while Nick Schmaltz finished with a goal and two assists for the Mammoth. Steven Stamkos scored a power-play goal 2:18 into overtime to lift Nashville over Colorado 3-2, his 247th career power-play goal tying Luc Robitaille for fifth on the NHL's all-time list.

Elsewhere, Connor McDavid extended his season-opening point streak to 17 games with a short-handed goal and three assists, but Edmonton still fell 5-4 to Anaheim. Boston beat Pittsburgh 6-2, Carolina topped the New York Islanders 5-3, the New York Rangers rallied past Philadelphia 3-2, and Tampa Bay stayed perfect at home with a 4-1 win over Chicago.

Reporting: Reuters
