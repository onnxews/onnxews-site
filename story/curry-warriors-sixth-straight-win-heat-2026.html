Curry Scores 22 After Halftime as Warriors Beat Heat 120-100 for Sixth Straight Win

Stephen Curry shook off a cold first half to score 29 points, and Golden State has not lost since its 0-3 start to the season.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Stephen Curry scored 22 of his game-high 29 points in a second-half surge as the Golden State Warriors won their sixth straight game Thursday night, defeating the Miami Heat 120-100 in San Francisco.

Curry shot just 2 of 10 from the field in the first half, but came alive after the break, shooting 7 of 9 including 5 of 7 from three-point range. The Warriors, who trailed briefly early in the third quarter, dominated from there with an 18-3 run featuring 11 points from Curry, and Miami never got closer than 10 points again.

Yaxel Lendeborg added 17 points, eight rebounds, six assists and three steals for Golden State, while Draymond Green chipped in a game-high 11 assists. The Warriors' bench contributed 53 points. Golden State has not lost since an 0-3 start and now sits at 6-3.

Miami was playing the second night of a back-to-back after needing overtime to earn a 117-116 road victory over the Los Angeles Clippers on Wednesday, a game Klay Thompson won with a last-second three-pointer. Thompson added 11 points for the Heat on Thursday in his first appearance at Chase Center as a member of the visiting team.

Davion Mitchell had a team-high 22 points for Miami, and Bam Adebayo added 18 points and 10 rebounds. Giannis Antetokounmpo finished with 20 points and a game-high 14 rebounds for the Heat, who fell to 3-6.
