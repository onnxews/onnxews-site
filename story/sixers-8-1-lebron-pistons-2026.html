LeBron, Sixers Improve to 8-1 With Road Win Over Pistons

The Sixers knocked off the Pistons on the road on Wednesday to improve to 8-1, with wins already over the Cavaliers and the defending champion Knicks twice.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

LeBron James and the Philadelphia 76ers are off to a great start to the 2026-27 season.

The Sixers knocked off the Detroit Pistons on the road on Wednesday, 112-106, to improve to 8-1 on the season. Among those eight wins, they have beaten the Pistons once, the Cleveland Cavaliers once, and the defending champion New York Knicks twice.

"We're nine games in, so this is a marathon," James said of the start. "For sure. Nine games in. 73 more to go, but this has been an impressive start for Philadelphia."

The Sixers will take on the Cavaliers on Friday to continue the road trip and continue their play in the NBA Cup as well.

Detroit fell to Philadelphia 106-112 in a high-scoring NBA regular season clash, with the Sixers' fast start now one of the early stories of the season.
