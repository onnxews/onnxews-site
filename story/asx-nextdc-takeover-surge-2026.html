ASX 200 Jumps 2.6% as NextDC Soars 51% on Takeover Offer

The ASX 200 posted its biggest gain in 19 months, led by materials and a 50 percent surge in data-center operator NextDC after a takeover offer.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Australia's S&P/ASX 200 closed at 9,065.1 on November 5, up 230.6 points or 2.61 percent, its largest one-day gain since April 10, 2025, after Democrats won the U.S. House and Senate and the Dow gained 3.03 percent.

Materials added about 151 basis points of the ASX 200's 261 basis points gain, and energy and financials took back about 27 basis points. The close was both the session high and the day's best level, after a low near 8,951.

The session's standout was NextDC, which rose 50.84 percent to $9.85 after a Goodman and Digital Realty vehicle offered $9.50 a share for up to 9.9 percent of the company. Market Index called it the data-center sector's best day since March 2020, after a fall of almost 20 percent from its August 25 record.

Energy was the weak spot, losing 4.76 percent, as January Brent settled 8.7 percent lower overnight at $94.62. December gold added 1.59 percent to $4,137.90 an ounce after a 3.43 percent rise overnight.

Australia's September goods surplus came in at A$2.83 billion, against an expected A$0.8 billion deficit and a revised A$0.18 billion surplus in August. S&P 500 futures were down 0.18 percent and Nasdaq 100 futures down 1.17 percent at 5:30 p.m. Sydney time.
