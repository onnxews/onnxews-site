Doncic Drops 53 as Lakers Rally, and Wembanyama Keeps the Spurs Perfect Streak Alive

Luka Doncic's fourth straight 40-point game powered a 124-118 win over Portland, while Victor Wembanyama's 30 points lifted San Antonio to a seventh straight victory.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Luka Doncic scored a season-high 53 points and Austin Reaves added 25 as the Los Angeles Lakers extended their winning streak to four games with a 124-118 victory over the visiting Portland Trail Blazers on Friday night. Doncic recorded his 11th career 50-point game, moving into a tie for 10th place on the NBA's all-time list, and his fourth consecutive game with at least 40 points.

The Lakers had to earn it. Portland built a 16-point lead and looked set to hand Los Angeles a second loss to the Blazers in a week, before the Lakers turned the game with a 17-0 run in the third quarter. Doncic's scoring carried the comeback, and the Lakers closed out the win to improve to 8-2, while the Trail Blazers saw their three-game winning streak end in just their second loss of the season.

Victor Wembanyama was just as dominant on the same night, scoring 30 points and grabbing 14 rebounds in just 26 minutes to lead the San Antonio Spurs past the Sacramento Kings 124-109. Wembanyama scored 20 of his 30 points in a runaway first half as the Spurs carried a 75-56 lead into halftime, and the Kings never got closer than 12 points the rest of the way.

The win was San Antonio's seventh straight, and it came two days after Wembanyama's career-high 52-point, 24-rebound, eight-block explosion in a one-point home win over the Dallas Mavericks. Stephon Castle was one of three Spurs players with 19 points against Sacramento, as the league's most exciting young team keeps finding new ways to win.

The rest of Friday's NBA Cup slate was full of statement results. The Cleveland Cavaliers snapped the Philadelphia 76ers' five-game winning streak with a 119-95 victory, holding LeBron James to eight points in the process. The Boston Celtics routed the Charlotte Hornets 119-80 behind a game-high 34 points from Jayson Tatum, and the Atlanta Hawks outlasted the Washington Wizards 139-132.

Oklahoma City rolled past Memphis 128-97 behind a career-high 35 points from Ajay Mitchell, Dallas topped Utah 112-100, and Toronto defeated Milwaukee 121-107 behind 27 points each from RJ Barrett and Alijah Martin, with Jakob Poeltl adding a 19-point, 11-rebound double-double. The loss dropped the Bucks to 1-8, and Sacramento matched them at 1-8, the worst nine-game start in franchise history.

The Minnesota Timberwolves erased a fourth-quarter deficit to beat the New Orleans Pelicans 115-109, halting New Orleans' four-game winning streak and extending their own to three. Nine NBA Cup group-play games filled the Friday schedule, including two national windows on Prime Video, and the results tightened several groups with the tournament still in its early stages.

Doncic's run is the story of the young season so far. Four straight games of at least 40 points, an 11th career 50-point night, and a Lakers team that keeps finding ways to win even when it falls behind by 16. The last player to sustain a scoring stretch like this had the league talking for weeks, and Doncic is doing it while his team climbs the Western Conference standings.

Saturday brings a new slate, but Friday belonged to the two young stars at the top of the league. Doncic and Wembanyama are playing the best basketball of their lives on the same nights, and the NBA is better for it. The only question now is how long either of them can keep it up.
