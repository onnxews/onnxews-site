Miley Cyrus Reportedly Set to Marry Maxx Morando This Weekend in Southern California

TMZ reported on Thursday that the singer and her fiance will exchange vows in Southern California, with details of the ceremony still under wraps.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 21, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Miley Cyrus is reportedly set to marry her fiance, Maxx Morando, this weekend in Southern California. TMZ reported on Thursday, Nov. 19, that sources with direct knowledge of the couple's plans said the singer and her rocker beau are set to tie the knot in Southern California. Details about the ceremony are still under wraps, and with the wedding days away, the couple are said to be gearing up to say I do.

The report has not been confirmed by the couple. Representatives for Cyrus and Morando did not immediately respond to requests for comment on Thursday afternoon, and no venue, exact date or guest list has been disclosed in the reporting so far. The wedding is therefore reported rather than confirmed, and the picture may change if the couple or their representatives speak publicly over the weekend.

The reported wedding comes nearly a year after the couple's engagement. Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, got engaged last year, and the singer later revealed that the proposal had caught her completely by surprise. She sparked engagement rumors when she was spotted wearing a diamond ring at the Avatar: Fire and Ash premiere in Los Angeles last December, and Page Six confirmed their engagement at the time.

Cyrus and Morando began dating in 2021, and their relationship has been conducted largely out of the spotlight compared with the singer's earlier romances. They met on a blind date, Cyrus revealed in a 2023 Vogue interview, and they soft-launched the romance with a photograph together at a Gucci event in November 2021. The following month they were spotted together at Miley's New Year's Eve Party in Miami, and they have been seen together regularly since.

The couple have also made music together, and Morando has been part of Cyrus's recent live performances. Last month, Cyrus brought Morando on stage at the Hollywood Bowl for a performance of their song Let's Get Married, a moment that now looks like a hint of what was to come. The performance was widely noted at the time, and TMZ pointed to it in its report on the wedding plans.

If the wedding goes ahead as reported, it will be Cyrus's second marriage. She was previously married to actor Liam Hemsworth, tying the knot in 2018 before the couple split less than a year later. That brief marriage was heavily covered at the time, and the contrast with her relationship with Morando, which has been kept far more private, has been a recurring theme in coverage of the couple.

There had already been hints that a wedding was close. During Cyrus's Hollywood Walk of Fame ceremony in May, her mother Tish Cyrus was reported to have blurted out, We're gonna bring the husband, while posing for photographs with Cyrus and her younger sister Brandi. The remark was taken at the time as a possible sign that the couple were already married or about to be, though nothing was confirmed then either.

For now, the only firm details in the reporting are the timing and the place: this weekend, in Southern California. Everything else, from the venue to the guest list to whether any of Cyrus's musical family members will perform, remains undisclosed. Fans will be watching for photographs or a statement from the couple over the weekend, but given how private the pair have kept their relationship, the ceremony may pass with little public detail at all.

Why it matters: The report matters because it shows how differently Cyrus is handling this chapter of her life from her first marriage. The Hemsworth wedding in 2018 was a major public event, dissected in real time, while the Morando relationship has been built slowly and largely in private, from a blind date in 2021 to a quiet engagement confirmed only after a ring was spotted at a premiere. A small, undisclosed ceremony in Southern California would fit that pattern, and it would also reflect a broader shift among celebrity couples toward keeping weddings out of the public eye. Until the couple confirm it themselves, though, the story remains a report based on sources, and the restraint in the reporting is itself part of the story.
