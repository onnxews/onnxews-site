Reaves Scores Season-High 43 as Lakers Beat Pistons Without Doncic

Reaves dropped a season-high 43 points as the Lakers won their fifth straight, 121-116 in Detroit, with Luka Doncic sidelined by lower back tightness.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 9, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Austin Reaves scored a season-high 43 points on Sunday to lead the Los Angeles Lakers to a 121-116 victory against the Detroit Pistons at Little Caesars Arena. The Lakers played without star guard Luka Doncic, who missed his second game due to tightness in his lower back, and still extended their winning streak to five games.

Reaves was superb from the start, shredding the league's best defense with jumpers and floaters and hitting four three-pointers on his way to 43 points. The Lakers set the tone early, building a 26-point lead, and although Detroit fought back to make it a game, Reaves had an answer every time the Pistons threatened.

Los Angeles improved to 9-2 with the win, and the victory was a statement about the depth of this roster. Doncic is the team's engine, and losing him for a second straight game could have derailed the road trip. Instead, Reaves stepped into the leading role and delivered the best scoring night of his season.

Detroit, which fell to 6-3, was led by Daniss Jenkins with 31 points, and Kevin Huerter added 24. The Pistons, who boast the league's best defense, had no answer for Reaves, and their comeback from 26 points down fell just short in the final minutes. The loss was their second in a row.

The Lakers' five-game winning streak has been built on more than one star. With Doncic sidelined, the supporting cast has carried the load, and the win in Detroit — against a strong defensive team, on the road, without their best player — was arguably the most impressive of the run. The Lakers continue their four-game road trip on Monday against the Atlanta Hawks.

Around the league on Sunday, the Philadelphia 76ers bounced back by defeating the Chicago Bulls 127-121, handing Chicago its seventh loss as Philadelphia secured its ninth victory in 11 outings. Tyrese Maxey led the way for the 76ers with 27 points, four rebounds and seven assists.

The Boston Celtics defeated the Milwaukee Bucks 117-97, the Los Angeles Clippers outlasted the New Orleans Pelicans 139-123 in a high-scoring matchup, and the Miami Heat claimed their fourth win of the season by beating the Sacramento Kings 114-108. The Minnesota Timberwolves beat the Utah Jazz 115-100, handing Utah its eighth straight loss, and the Dallas Mavericks routed the Brooklyn Nets 130-93 behind a triple-double from Cooper Flagg, who had 25 points, five rebounds and 12 assists.

For the Lakers, the question now is when Doncic returns. The back tightness has cost him two games, and the team will not want to rush him back with the season still young. But the win in Detroit showed that this roster can survive without him — and that Austin Reaves, on nights like this one, can carry a team by himself.

Reaves's 43 points were a season high, but the number that matters most is 9-2. The Lakers are winning, the streak is five, and they are doing it with or without their star. That is the mark of a team with real ambitions this season.
