Wembanyama Drops 52 Points, 24 Rebounds and 8 Blocks as Spurs Edge Mavs 112-111

Wembanyama put up 52 points, 24 rebounds and 8 blocks as the Spurs edged the Mavericks by a point. Jokic added a triple-double for Denver the same night.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

In a thrilling finish, San Antonio narrowly defeated Dallas 112-111 on Wednesday night, and Victor Wembanyama dominated with 52 points, 24 rebounds and 8 blocks.

Dylan Harper scored 21 points, and Stephon Castle contributed 17 points and 10 rebounds for the Spurs. Dallas's Kyrie Irving led with 29 points and 7 rebounds, playing 36 minutes after sitting out Monday's loss to the Kings.

It was one of the great individual nights of the young NBA season: a 52-point, 24-rebound, 8-block line from the Spurs' star, in a one-point game decided in the closing seconds.

Elsewhere on Wednesday, Nikola Jokic recorded a triple-double with 32 points, 10 rebounds and 11 assists as Denver secured a 128-109 win over Toronto. Oklahoma City defeated Cleveland 124-116, New York dominated Chicago 135-105, and Portland beat Phoenix 132-119.

The night also produced a one-point thriller on the West Coast, where Klay Thompson put up 30 points as the Miami Heat beat the Los Angeles Clippers 117-116.
