Zelensky Awards Melania Trump One of Ukraine's Highest Honors for Oleshky Truce

The first lady received the Order of Princess Olga for her role in the six-day ceasefire that evacuated about 800 civilians from the Russian-held town. She hosts Russian and Ukrainian officials at the White House on Thursday.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky has awarded U.S. first lady Melania Trump a state honor for her role in helping to organize a humanitarian evacuation from a frontline town last week, according to a decree published on Wednesday.

The presidential decree, dated November 2, cited her significant personal contribution to humanitarian support for Ukraine and her work to help Ukrainian children, including those deported or forcibly moved from Russian-occupied territory.

The award, the Order of Princess Olga, 1st class, honors women who have made exceptional contributions in spheres including science, education, culture and charitable work.

Ukrainian officials have said Melania Trump played a pivotal role in organizing the six-day ceasefire to evacuate civilians from the Russian-occupied southern Ukrainian town of Oleshky, which began on October 27 and concluded on November 1. It was the longest local pause in fighting since Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022, and nearly 800 people were evacuated.

On Thursday, November 5, Melania Trump is scheduled to meet Russian and Ukrainian representatives at the White House, where she will deliver remarks on the Oleshky mission, according to the White House. Ukraine hopes to replicate the humanitarian mission in other occupied territories, Le Monde reported.

Reporting: Reuters
