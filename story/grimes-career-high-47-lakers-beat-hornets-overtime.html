Grimes' Career-High 47 Lifts Lakers Past Hornets 118–114 in Overtime

Quentin Grimes scored 21 points in the fourth quarter and hit the tying three with two seconds left as the Lakers won their ninth straight game.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 18, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Quentin Grimes scored a career-high 47 points on Tuesday night, including the game-tying three-pointer with about two seconds left in regulation, as the Los Angeles Lakers rallied from 18 points down to beat the Charlotte Hornets 118–114 in overtime at Crypto.com Arena. The win was the Lakers' ninth in a row and kept them at the top of the NBA at 13–2.

Grimes was the story of the night. He shot 16-for-24 from the field, including 10-for-14 from three-point range, and his 10 threes made him only the second Laker ever to make at least 10 in a game, alongside Kobe Bryant. It was his third 40-point game of his NBA career, and his previous career high was 46 points, set on March 17, 2025 while he was with the Philadelphia 76ers.

The Lakers needed every bit of it. They trailed for most of the night and were down by 18 late in the third quarter, with the Hornets leading 94–81 after three. With Luka Doncic on the bench for the first six minutes of the fourth quarter, Grimes scored 21 points in the period, dragging Los Angeles back into a game that had looked lost.

The finish was frantic. With the Lakers down 110–106 and 20 seconds left, Grimes made a layup to cut the deficit to two. Coby White split a pair of free throws for Charlotte, and then Grimes buried his 10th three of the night, on an assist from Doncic, to tie the game at 111–111 and force overtime. At 45 points, he was one short of his career high.

Doncic had a quieter night by his standards but still finished with 36 points, seven assists and six rebounds on 13-for-34 shooting, and his 11th consecutive 30-point game extended a historic early-season run. Elgin Baylor is the only other Laker with multiple streaks of at least 11 consecutive 30-point games. Doncic entered Tuesday leading the NBA with 39.0 points per game, along with 7.8 assists and 7.2 rebounds.

Together, Grimes and Doncic combined for 83 points and 15 threes, and the Lakers outscored the Hornets 37–20 across the fourth quarter and overtime, holding Charlotte to just three points in the extra period. Doncic's fadeaway at the 3:15 mark of overtime gave Los Angeles its first lead since the second quarter.

Grimes delivered the final blow as well. With 17 seconds left in overtime and the Lakers up 115–114, he forced a turnover on the Hornets' inbounds pass to Royce O'Neale, was intentionally fouled and made both free throws to reach 47 points and put Los Angeles up by three. Matisse Thybulle forced another turnover on the ensuing possession and made a free throw to complete the 118–114 final.

Charlotte was led by Brandon Miller with 28 points and Coby White with 19, but the Hornets kept Miller and White on the bench throughout overtime and fell to 4–10, their ninth loss in 10 games. The Lakers were without Austin Reaves, out for a second straight game with a heel injury, and Walker Kessler, who was ruled out ahead of tipoff, and they needed their two stars to carry the load.

The Lakers' winning streak is now tied for their longest under head coach JJ Redick, and they close their home back-to-back set against the Utah Jazz on Wednesday. USA Today and the New York Post both covered the game on Tuesday night.

Why it matters: This was a win the Lakers had no business getting, and that is what makes it the most revealing game of their nine-game streak. They were down 18 points in the third quarter, missing two starters, with their superstar in foul trouble and shooting 13-for-34, and they still won because Quentin Grimes played the game of his life. A career-high 47 points, 10 threes and the two biggest defensive plays of the night is not a role player's hot hand, it is a second star announcing himself. If Grimes can do this when Doncic is struggling, the Lakers are no longer a one-man team — and that changes their ceiling for the season.
