Coachella 2027: Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA to Headline, With Madonna in the Dance Tent

Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA will headline the two April weekends, with Madonna back in the dance tent and Daddy Yankee billed 'in the desert.'

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Coachella has unveiled its 2027 lineup, and the desert festival's next edition will be headlined by Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA, with Daddy Yankee receiving special top billing and Madonna returning to the dance tent. The announcement, made late on November 10, ended months of speculation and set up what the festival's promoter, Goldenvoice, is calling 'The return to innocence.'

The festival returns to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, across two weekends: April 9 to 11 and April 16 to 18, 2027. Dua Lipa will headline Friday, Tame Impala will headline Saturday and SZA will close out Sunday on both weekends. Daddy Yankee, the reggaeton star known as the King of Reggaeton, gets a billing all his own, listed as 'in the desert,' while Madonna will perform a set in the dance tent on Friday night.

Madonna's appearance is the biggest surprise on the poster. It will be her return to the festival's dance tent 21 years after her celebrated 2006 appearance, and it places a career of pop reinvention inside the electronic music program. Daddy Yankee's billing is also notable: he played farewell shows in 2023 before returning to music in 2025, and this will be his festival debut in the desert.

The headliners are all reaching the top of the bill at significant moments. Dua Lipa will make her Coachella headline debut, a decade after appearing as a surprise guest with Martin Garrix in 2017. SZA will also make her headline debut, closing each weekend after months of hints, including an appearance on social media that seemed to acknowledge the booking and previewed an unreleased track. Tame Impala returns to the top of the bill after previously headlining in 2019.

The undercard runs deep. Saturday brings Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiesto, Martin Garrix, Robyn, Role Model, Maneskin, Underworld, Leon Bridges, Charlotte de Witte, Magdalena Bay, NCT, Enigma and more. Sunday features Don Toliver, Dom Dolla, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, Four Tet, The Neighbourhood, Becky G, BABYMONSTER, Amelie Lens, Sara Landry, Remi Wolf, Tove Lo, Victoria Monet, EsDeeKid, Caroline Polachek, Chase and Status, MUNA, Boris Brejcha and Jorja Smith.

Passes go on sale Friday, November 13, at noon Pacific time, with general admission at Tier 2 pricing of 779 dollars including a three-day shuttle pass, and VIP starting at 1,399 dollars for the first weekend. YouTube will livestream both weekends of the festival exclusively, as it has in recent years, giving fans who cannot get to the desert a front-row seat at home.

The 2027 headliners follow a star-studded 2026 lineup that saw Justin Bieber, Sabrina Carpenter, Karol G and Anyma lead the weekend. The three new headliners match what TMZ reported on October 30, when the outlet named Dua Lipa, SZA and Tame Impala as the 2027 headliners before the festival had said anything, and the official poster confirms all three.

For the festival business, the lineup is a statement of intent. Coachella remains the largest and most influential music festival in North America, and its poster sets the tone for the entire festival season. A bill led by two first-time headliners and a returning psychedelic rock act, with Madonna and Daddy Yankee in special slots, is a bet on both the present and the history of pop music.

The announcement also shows how the festival has changed. Where Coachella once leaned on rock headliners, the 2027 top line spans global pop, psychedelic rock, alternative R and B and reggaeton, with electronic music given a prime showcase through Madonna's dance tent set. It is a lineup built for streaming as much as for the desert, with acts whose audiences live as much on phones as in festival fields.

Tickets will be the next test. Passes have sold out in hours in recent years, and the combination of three hugely popular headliners and a Madonna return is likely to make the November 13 sale one of the fastest in the festival's history. Fans who miss out will have the livestream, but for the desert itself, the race starts Friday at noon.
