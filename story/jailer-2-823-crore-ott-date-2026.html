'Jailer 2' Tops Rs 823 Crore Worldwide, Now the Highest-Grossing Tamil Film Ever

Rajinikanth's blockbuster completed three weeks in cinemas with a record haul, and Prime Video has confirmed it will start streaming on November 12.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 has completed three weeks at the box office with 823.50 crore rupees in worldwide gross, already becoming the highest-grossing Tamil film of all time, according to industry tracker Sacnilk.

The Sun Pictures production has surpassed Rajinikanth's previous blockbuster 2.0 to claim both the India and worldwide gross records for Tamil cinema. Its India gross stands at 546.95 crore rupees after 22 days, with overseas markets contributing 276.50 crore rupees.

Collections are now fading fast as new releases take over screens. On Thursday, November 5, the film collected 1.85 crore rupees in India gross, a drop of about 47 percent from the previous day, while its tracked shows fell from 8,004 to 2,976.

Prime Video has officially confirmed November 12 as the film's streaming premiere date, ending exhibitors' hopes of an extended theatrical window. The film will stream in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada in India and more than 240 countries and territories.

The arrival of new releases, including Ramayana, has reshuffled the theatrical market and cut into Jailer 2's remaining screens, particularly in Tamil Nadu and other southern markets where the film had generated most of its remaining business.

Reporting: Sacnilk
