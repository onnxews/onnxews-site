Spider-Man and BTS Sweep the 2026 Kids' Choice Awards

Spider-Man: Brand New Day won four movie prizes and BTS took three music awards at the 2026 Kids' Choice Awards on Saturday night, the first to air simultaneously on CBS and Nickelodeon.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 15, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Spider-Man and BTS swept the 2026 Kids' Choice Awards on Saturday night, November 14, in a ceremony that put the year's biggest family film and its biggest music act at the centre of Nickelodeon's annual celebration. The show was held at Television City in Los Angeles, hosted by the singer Alex Warren, and aired live from 8:00 to 9:30 p.m. ET on CBS and Nickelodeon at the same time for the first time, with premium Paramount+ subscribers able to stream it live as well.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day was the night's biggest movie winner, taking Favorite Movie and three of the four movie acting prizes. Tom Holland won Favorite Movie Actor and Zendaya won Favorite Movie Actress, while Sadie Sink won Favorite Villain for playing Jean Grey — four movie-category wins for a single film. The sweep reflected the film's dominance with the young audiences who vote for the awards, and it gave the night its clearest storyline.

BTS matched the film's haul on the music side, winning three awards. The group took Favorite Music Group or Duo for the fifth time, a record in the category, along with Favorite Song for "SWIM" and Favorite Album for ARIRANG. The three wins underlined the group's continued hold on the awards even as its members have pursued solo work, and the fifth group win put them further ahead of every other act in the show's history.

Chris Pratt received the night's special honour, the King of Comedy award, and used his acceptance speech to joke about his own family. "I love kids! … I have four kids of my own, and them being born was the greatest thing that ever happened to me, until tonight!" he told the crowd. The award recognised a career built largely on family films, from animated features to the blockbusters that made him a household name with young audiences.

The rest of the winners covered the full range of what children and teenagers watch, play and listen to. Zootopia 2 won Favorite Animated Movie, Percy Jackson and the Olympians won Favorite Kids TV Show, and Stranger Things won Favorite Family TV Show. ROSÉ won Favorite Female Artist and Bruno Mars won Favorite Male Artist, while Lionel Messi won Favorite Male Sports Star and Alysa Liu won Favorite Female Sports Star. Roblox won Favorite Video Game and MrBeast won Favorite Male Creator.

The first CBS simulcast was itself a milestone for the show. The ceremony aired live on CBS and Nickelodeon at the same time for the first time, with premium Paramount+ subscribers able to stream it live. Nickelodeon's awards have traditionally aired only on its own channels, and putting them on broadcast television at the same time brought the ceremony to an audience that might never have tuned in on cable. The move reflects how much the awards have become a showcase for the whole of the Paramount business, from its films to its streaming service.

Taylor Swift led the nominations with six, though the night's biggest prizes went elsewhere, and the voting — by children and teenagers — once again rewarded the acts and films that dominate their feeds. The awards are decided by fan votes rather than by an academy, which is why a group like BTS can win three times and a film like Spider-Man can sweep its categories: the winners are the ones young viewers actually care about.

Warren's hosting kept the night moving between the awards and the performances, and the ceremony's mix of slime, celebrity appearances and fan-voted prizes remained intact despite the move to a bigger stage. The show has kept the same basic formula for decades — children vote, stars get slimed, and the biggest names in family entertainment turn up — and the 2026 edition did not change it.

The next test for the awards will be whether the CBS simulcast becomes permanent. If the ratings justify it, Nickelodeon will have found a way to put its biggest night in front of a broadcast audience every year, and the show's winners will matter to an even wider group of viewers than they already do.

Why it matters: The night mattered because it showed where young audiences actually are: Spider-Man sweeping the movie categories and BTS winning three music awards is a snapshot of what children and teenagers watched and listened to this year, not what critics or academies chose. The first CBS simulcast also matters — Nickelodeon put its biggest night on broadcast television for the first time, betting that the awards can reach beyond its own channels. If the ratings hold, the simulcast will become a fixture, and the show's fan-voted winners will carry more weight with the wider industry.
