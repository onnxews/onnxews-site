Netflix Orders Harlan Coben's The Innocent as Its 15th Adaptation of the Author

The streamer ordered a limited series of Coben's 2005 novel at the Texas Book Festival, its 15th adaptation of the author's work.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 18, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Netflix has ordered a limited series based on Harlan Coben's 2005 novel The Innocent, the company's 15th adaptation of the bestselling thriller author's work. The order was announced on Monday, November 16, at Netflix's first appearance at the Texas Book Festival in Austin, which ran from November 14 to 15.

The series will be run by Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien, the team behind I Will Find You, who will write and executive produce. Their production company, StoryMath, has signed an overall deal to create new series exclusively for Netflix. Harlan Coben and his son Ben Coben will executive produce through their company, Final Twist Productions. No cast has been announced and no release date has been set.

The novel follows Matt Hunter, a man whose mysterious past resurfaces after he and his wife buy a home, leaving him accused of killing people in his hometown and fighting to prove his innocence. It is the kind of twist-driven premise that has made Coben's books a reliable source of streaming hits: an ordinary man, a buried secret and a trail of suspicion that keeps widening.

This is the second time Netflix has adapted The Innocent. A Spanish-language version in 2021 starred Mario Casas and holds a perfect critics score on Rotten Tomatoes, with a 77% audience score. The new version will be the English-language take on the same book, and the 15th Coben adaptation in total for the streamer.

The partnership has become one of Netflix's most dependable engines. "Our partnership with Harlan Coben is one of the greatest creative relationships at Netflix, and The Innocent marks our 15th adaptation together," said Cole Galvin, Netflix's vice president of drama series, adding that nobody builds a "can't-look-away thriller" like Harlan. Hull, Wynbrandt and Lilien said in a statement that they were "thrilled to call Netflix our home" and "excited to bring our signature brand of twisty and propulsive storytelling to The Innocent and the many new stories we look forward to telling at Netflix."

The numbers explain why Netflix keeps coming back. Coben's shows have made the weekly Global Top 10 41 times since 2021, reached the top 10 in 93 countries and drawn more than 450 million views since 2023. Fool Me Once alone accounts for more than 98 million views. I Will Find You, released in June 2026 with Sam Worthington as David Burroughs, finished at number five on Netflix's Most Popular list with 120.8 million views in 91 days, and sales of the book rose by more than 500% after the June 1 teaser.

The adaptation pipeline is part of a wider bet on books. Netflix says book adaptations have generated more than 13 billion views and nearly 20% of all viewing hours since 2025, and a book adaptation has featured in the global top 10s for 98% of 2026 so far. The Innocent is the latest in a line that has made Coben the streamer's most adapted living author.

The festival brought other announcements as well. The second season of 3 Body Problem arrives on Thursday, January 14, 2027, with six episodes, down from eight in the first season. Myron Bolitar is in production in New York, and The Woods wrapped filming this fall. Coben signed an overall deal with Netflix in 2018, after the series Safe, starring Michael Hall, and he also hosts the true-crime series Harlan Coben's Final Twist on CBS, with new episodes on Monday nights. What's-on-Netflix and ScreenRant both reported the order this week.

Why it matters: Fifteen adaptations of one author is not a programming strategy, it is a franchise, and Netflix's Coben pipeline has become one of the most reliable engines in streaming. The numbers — 450 million views since 2023, a book adaptation in the global top 10 for 98% of this year — show why the streamer keeps ordering more: these are cheap to market, easy to binge and proven to travel to 93 countries. The Innocent is also a test of whether the formula still has room to grow, since this is the second adaptation of the same book. If the English-language version lands the way the Spanish one did, it will confirm that for Netflix, a good Coben twist is effectively a renewable resource.
