GHOSTBUSTERS TANGO AND A FIRST-EVER TRIPLE DANCE-OFF: STRICTLY'S HALLOWEEN SPECIAL

Strictly Come Dancing's Halloween Special airs Saturday night with 11 couples dancing everything from a Ghostbusters tango to a Little Mix quickstep — and Sunday brings the first triple dance-off in the show's history.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 31, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Strictly Come Dancing's Halloween Special airs on BBC One and iPlayer on Saturday, October 31, from 6:30 p.m. to 8:30 p.m., with 11 couples dancing everything from a Ghostbusters tango to a Little Mix quickstep.

Lawrence Robb and Jowita Przystał dance a tango to 'Ghostbusters' by Ray Parker Jr., while Bethany Antonia and Nancy Xu jive to Fifth Harmony's 'I'm in Love with a Monster.'

The results show follows on Sunday, November 1 at 7:10 p.m., featuring a Dracula-themed professional group dance with Sir Ian McKellen's specially recorded voice reading from Bram Stoker's Dracula.

Sunday's results show will also feature the first triple dance-off in Strictly history to decide who goes home.

The Halloween special is one of the series' biggest annual set pieces, with elaborate costumes and themed routines across the night.
