THEE CIRCUS IS HERE: Megan Thee Stallion's Hottieween Takes Over Vegas Tonight

Megan Thee Stallion's annual Hottieween party takes over Zouk Nightclub in Las Vegas tonight — a circus-themed blowout with Cirque du Soleil, special guest Uncle Waffles, and costumes mandatory.

By Peter Osibajo · Orbit News Network · Oct 30, 2026 · 3 min read

LAS VEGAS — Megan Thee Stallion's annual Hottieween party takes over Zouk Nightclub in Las Vegas tonight, a circus-themed blowout built for Halloween weekend.

This year's edition brings Cirque du Soleil into the mix, with special guest Uncle Waffles on the bill — and costumes mandatory for everyone walking through the door.

The creepy black-and-white trailer for the party dropped Wednesday, setting the tone for a night that leans fully into the circus theme rather than a standard club set.

A portion of proceeds from the night benefits Megan's Pete & Thomas Foundation, the organization she founded in honor of her parents, which supports education, housing and health initiatives.

Hottieween has become an annual fixture for Megan's fans, the Hotties, who treat the Halloween party as one of the biggest nights on her calendar — a costume party, a concert and a full production in one.

Tonight's Vegas edition is the latest stop in that tradition, on Halloween eve itself, with the circus theme giving the party its biggest stage yet.
