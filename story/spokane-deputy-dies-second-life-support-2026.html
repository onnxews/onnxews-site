One Spokane County Deputy Dead, Second on Life Support After Ambush at Domestic Call

The two young SWAT deputies were shot trying to enter a home near Spangle after a woman reported her brother had shot her. Four people are dead, including the suspect.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

One of two sheriff's deputies wounded in a firefight in Washington state has died, and the other is not expected to survive, authorities said Wednesday.

Deputies responded to a home in Spangle, a small farming community south of Spokane, on Tuesday afternoon after a woman told her neighbor she had been shot by her brother, the Spokane County Sheriff's Office said. When a SWAT team moved to breach the door, a gunman inside opened fire.

Both deputies were struck and taken to a hospital in critical condition. One died overnight, and the other was being kept on life support on Wednesday, allowing for the donation of his organs, the sheriff's office said in a written statement. "He will continue to serve others with the gift of life," the statement said.

The deputy who died is survived by his wife and two children. Neither deputy has been identified, and their families have asked for privacy. Sheriff John Nowels described both as younger members of the SWAT team.

Other deputies returned fire and found the suspect dead inside, along with one other victim in the house and one in the yard, authorities said. The woman who reported being shot by her brother was hospitalized with injuries that were not life-threatening. Spokane police are investigating the deaths, and the Washington State Patrol is reviewing the deputies' use of force.
