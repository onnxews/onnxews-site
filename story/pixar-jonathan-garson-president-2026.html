Pixar Names Jonathan Garson President as Jim Morris Steps Back

Jonathan Garson takes over on January 4, with Pete Docter staying as chief creative officer. Jim Morris, president for 12 years, becomes president emeritus.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Pixar Animation Studios said on Wednesday that veteran Jonathan Garson will become president on January 4, replacing Jim Morris, who will step back from day-to-day leadership ahead of his eventual retirement.

The reshuffle sets up the next generation of leadership at Pixar, with Garson taking charge of production and operations while Chief Creative Officer Pete Docter retains oversight of the studio's films, the studio said. Jonas Rivera has been elevated to executive vice president of creative.

Morris, Pixar's president for 12 years, will step back to take the president emeritus role and remain involved in "Incredibles 3" and other projects. Garson has been with Pixar since 2010 and with Disney for more than 30 years.

The changes were announced by Alan Bergman, chairman of Disney Entertainment, Studios at The Walt Disney Company. Garson and Morris will both report to Bergman.

Pixar's upcoming slate includes "Gatto" in March 2027, "Ghost Market" in March 2028 and "Incredibles 3" in June 2028. The studio most recently released the billion-dollar hit "Toy Story 5."

Reporting: Reuters
