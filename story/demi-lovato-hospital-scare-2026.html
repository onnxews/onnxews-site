DEMI LOVATO SPEAKS OUT AFTER BRIEF HOSPITAL SCARE

The singer says she had a brief hospital visit after a possible allergic reaction — possibly from passionfruit or sushi, though the exact trigger is not confirmed. She reassured fans she is doing fine after being discharged.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 29, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Demi Lovato says she had a brief hospital visit after a possible allergic reaction, and has reassured fans she is doing fine after being discharged.

Lovato shared a photo showing an IV and a hospital wristband, telling fans about the scare herself before speculation could fill the gap.

Lovato shared a photo showing an IV and a hospital wristband, telling fans about the scare herself before speculation could fill the gap.

The possible trigger was passionfruit or sushi, according to Lovato's account — though the exact cause is not confirmed, and Lovato did not present it as a diagnosis.

Lovato later deleted the posts, after the initial message had already spread and fans had responded with concern.

What remains clear from Lovato's own account is the outcome: a brief visit, a discharge, and a reassurance that she is doing fine.

No further medical detail has been shared, and the episode, as Lovato described it, was short — a scare, not a longer hospital stay.
