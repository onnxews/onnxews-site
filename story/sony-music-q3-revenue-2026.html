Sony Music Revenue Hits $3.2 Billion in Q3 as Streaming Jumps

Recorded music revenue jumped 19.5 percent to $2.42 billion, with streaming up 16 percent. Ateez led the quarter's releases, and Sony raised its full-year music forecast.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Sony Music generated nearly $3.2 billion during the third quarter of 2026, up 17.7 percent year over year, and 8.9 percent in constant currency, according to Sony Group's latest earnings report.

Recorded music kicked in the lion's share of the sum at $2.42 billion, up 19.5 percent year over year, with streaming accounting for $1.53 billion, up 16.1 percent. Live events, merchandising and licensing also contributed to the growth.

By revenue, the quarter's top-performing releases were Ateez's "Golden Hour: Part 5," Michael Jackson's "Thriller," Ella Langley's "Dandelion," Bad Bunny's "DeBI TiRAR MaS FOToS," SZA's "SOS" and Shakira's "Dai Dai," respectively.

Looking ahead to the remainder of the fiscal year, meaning the rest of 2026 and then 2027's first quarter, the conglomerate upped its music-segment revenue forecast by about 5 percent to $14.45 billion.

Music sales rose about 10 percent on gains in live events, merchandising and streaming, and operating income increased 6 percent. Michael Jackson's "Thriller" remained a top seller as interest from the biopic continued.
