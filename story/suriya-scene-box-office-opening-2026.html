Suriya's Scene Opens Big With Rs 34 Crore Worldwide on Day One

The action-comedy outpaced both of the star's earlier 2026 releases on opening day, leading a crowded Diwali weekend at the Indian box office.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Suriya's latest film Scene has made a strong start at the box office, crossing the Rs 30 crore mark worldwide on its opening day. The action-comedy, directed by Jithu Madhavan, collected Rs 19.15 crore net at the Indian box office on Friday, November 6, translating to a gross of Rs 22.25 crore, according to Sacnilk, with another Rs 12 crore coming from overseas markets for a worldwide total of Rs 34.25 crore.

The opening is the biggest of Suriya's year so far. His earlier 2026 release Karuppu opened to Rs 21.93 crore worldwide on its first day, while Vishwanath and Sons grossed Rs 27.51 crore on day one. Scene has beaten both, and the overseas contribution, more than a third of the worldwide total, shows the star's drawing power beyond India.

The Tamil version did the heavy lifting at home, earning Rs 16.15 crore net from 4,878 shows, while the Telugu version added Rs 3 crore from 1,289 shows. Overall occupancy stood at 55 percent across 6,167 shows, a healthy figure for a non-holiday Friday and a sign that audiences turned out in force for the opening.

Day two is holding up. On Saturday, Scene collected an estimated Rs 4.88 crore net in India in early reporting, taking its total India net collection to Rs 24.03 crore, with the Tamil version contributing Rs 4.07 crore and the Telugu version adding Rs 81 lakh. The weekend will decide whether the film can build on its opening or settles into a more modest run.

The film arrived in the middle of a crowded Diwali window, and the competition has been no contest so far. Nayanthara's Mookuthi Amman 2, which opened on the same day, collected just Rs 1.88 crore net in India on its first day, with a gross of Rs 2.15 crore, and its two-day total stands at only Rs 2.41 crore. Scene is outgrossing its direct rival by roughly ten to one.

Ramayana remains the bigger beast in the market. Nitesh Tiwari's epic, which arrived in theaters a day earlier on Thursday, November 5, collected Rs 64.85 crore net on its opening day and another Rs 48.05 crore on Friday, and it is surging again on Saturday. Scene does not need to beat Ramayana to succeed, but it does need to hold its screens through the weekend.

Scene marks Suriya's third film release this year, an unusually busy stretch for one of Tamil cinema's biggest stars. The film pairs him with Naslen in the lead roles, and composer Sushin Shyam makes his Tamil debut with the project, adding a fresh musical voice to a film that leans on comedy and action in equal measure.

The Diwali window is one of the most important release periods in Indian cinema, and this year's slate is unusually packed. A strong opening day is only the first test: the Saturday and Sunday collections will determine whether Scene has the legs to become one of the season's big winners or fades once the holiday crowds move on.

For now, Suriya has the opening he wanted, comfortably ahead of his own recent films and far ahead of the weekend's other new release. The next two days will tell whether Scene can turn a big start into a big run.
