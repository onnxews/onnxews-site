FROM THE ER TO SEXIEST MAN ALIVE: Noah Wyle Takes People's 2026 Title

The Pitt star and former ER doctor is People's Sexiest Man Alive for 2026. He thought the whole thing was a prank.

POPeter OsibajoOrbit News Network · Oct 27, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Noah Wyle is People's Sexiest Man Alive for 2026. The magazine announced the honor Tuesday, and the 55-year-old actor's first reaction was disbelief.

In a million years I did not think this would be an honor bestowed on me, Wyle said. He thought the whole thing was a prank.

The title caps a remarkable second act. Wyle first became a household name as Dr. John Carter on ER, the NBC medical drama that defined Thursday nights in the 1990s and 2000s. Fifteen seasons, one of the biggest shows in TV history, and a character audiences watched grow from wide-eyed med student to attending physician.

Then came The Pitt. As Dr. Robby Robinavitch on the Max medical drama, Wyle returned to the ER — older, wearier, and carrying the weight of a career spent in trauma bays. The show became a critical hit and a streaming favorite, and it put Wyle back at the center of the culture.

He takes over the title from Jonathan Bailey, continuing a run of winners that reflects whatever Hollywood is feeling in a given year. This year, apparently, it is feeling a 55-year-old TV doctor.

The honor is, by design, a little silly — and Wyle seems to know it. But there is something fitting about it too: after three decades in the business, the guy who spent his career saving fictional lives is finally getting his flowers in real life.

The Pitt's success is part of a broader TV trend: prestige medical dramas built for streaming, with long seasons and ensemble casts. Wyle's Dr. Robby anchors the show the way Carter once anchored ER — the steady center of a chaotic ward.

People's Sexiest Man Alive has been running since 1985, and the title has gone to movie stars, musicians, and TV leads. Wyle's win is a reminder that the franchise still rewards longevity: the actor audiences grew up with is, three decades later, still the one they want to watch.
