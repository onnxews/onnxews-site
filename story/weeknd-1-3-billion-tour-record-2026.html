The Weeknd Wraps $1.3 Billion Tour, the Highest-Grossing Ever by a Male Artist

The After Hours Til Dawn stadium tour spanned 176 shows across five continents and passed Elton John's record, landing behind only Taylor Swift and Coldplay on the all-time list.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

The Weeknd has closed out his After Hours Til Dawn stadium tour with a record-setting tally of more than $1.3 billion, making it the highest-grossing tour by a male artist in history. The final show took place Wednesday night at TM Stadium National in Kuala Lumpur.

The tour, confirmed by promoter Live Nation, spanned 176 shows across 67 cities, 30 countries and five continents since beginning in July 2022, selling more than 9 million tickets. The 2026 leg alone brought in more than $500 million from over 3 million tickets.

The Weeknd surpasses Elton John, whose Farewell Yellow Brick Road tour earned $939 million from 330 shows between 2018 and 2023. The achievement ranks as the third-biggest concert tour in history, behind only Taylor Swift's Eras Tour, with $2 billion in revenue, and Coldplay's Music of the Spheres tour, at more than $1.5 billion.

Along the way, The Weeknd set venue records around the world, including the most performances by a male solo artist at Rogers Centre in Toronto, the most performances and highest gross by a male artist at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, and the first six sold-out nights at Stade de France in Paris on a single tour.

The tour also raised $8.5 million for the XO Humanitarian Fund and Global Citizen, supporting hunger relief and access to quality education for children worldwide.

Reporting: USA Today
