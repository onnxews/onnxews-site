Grizzlies Rout Short-Handed Warriors 126-98 for Fifth Straight Win

Jaylen Wells scored 22 points and Memphis never trailed as Golden State finished a winless five-game road trip without the injured Stephen Curry.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 20, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Jaylen Wells scored a season-high-tying 22 points and the Memphis Grizzlies ran up a 126-98 victory over the Golden State Warriors on Thursday night at FedExForum, extending their winning streak to five games and completing a miserable road trip for the visitors.

Wells buried four of his five three-point attempts, and Cedric Coward went 5-for-9 from deep on a 19-point night for Memphis. The Grizzlies outscored the Warriors 45-15 from beyond the arc, hitting 42.9 percent of their three-pointers while Golden State connected on just 5 of 29, or 17.2 percent.

Memphis never trailed. The Grizzlies scored the game's first five points, led 31-24 after the first quarter and built the lead to 52-34 midway through the second. Golden State closed to within 55-47 at halftime, but Memphis scored nine straight points early in the third period and coasted from there, leading by 34 points in the fourth quarter.

Cameron Boozer and Olivier-Maxence Prosper scored 16 points apiece for the Grizzlies, Javon Small added 14, and Cam Spencer directed the attack with a game-high 11 assists to go with nine points. Memphis outscored Golden State 71-51 in the second half.

The game also featured a duel between two of the league's standout rookies. Boozer, the third overall pick in the 2026 draft, finished 6-for-6 from the field with six rebounds and five assists, while Golden State's Yaxel Lendeborg, the 11th pick, produced 11 points, eight rebounds and seven assists. The pair were cited by the NBA before the game as the first rookies to average at least 16 points, seven rebounds and three assists through their first 15 games since Larry Bird and Magic Johnson in 1979-80.

Golden State's problems start with injuries. The Warriors lost Stephen Curry to an oblique tear in the first quarter of their trip opener at Houston last Wednesday, and he has now missed four consecutive games. Draymond Green also sat out in Memphis with a right elbow injury, and the injury-plagued Warriors used their 12th different starting lineup of the season.

Charles Bassey was Golden State's surprise leading scorer with 16 points and a game-high 13 rebounds, while Brandin Podziemski and Will Richard totalled 15 points apiece. But the Warriors have now lost five straight games, the last four by a combined 109 points, and they finished the road trip 0-5.

Memphis had injury problems of its own. The Grizzlies were without starters Ty Jerome, the team's leading scorer, and centre Zach Edey, and they lost Jerami Grant early in the second quarter after he landed awkwardly on his left leg following a three-pointer. The depth of the roster covered for all of it.

The win moved Memphis to 12-4 on the season, while Golden State fell to 8-8. It was also the first time Memphis has beaten Golden State twice in a season since 2022-23, a measure of how far the balance of this matchup has shifted. The Grizzlies are 4-4 at home this season and will try to open the season with a ninth straight road victory when they visit Minnesota on Saturday.

The Warriors, meanwhile, return home to host Boston on Sunday, hoping that rest and their own building can stop a slide that has turned a promising start into a .500 record. Until Curry returns, though, the offence that once defined this team looks a long way from itself.

Why it matters: This game was a tale of two injury lists, and Memphis showed what depth is for. The Grizzlies were missing their leading scorer and their starting centre and still won by 28, because six players reached double figures and the ball moved for 34 assists. Golden State, by contrast, has no answer for life without Curry: five straight losses, a winless road trip and a three-point attack that went 5-for-29. The Warriors' season now depends on how quickly Curry's oblique heals, while Memphis looks like a team that can win in more than one way.
