'Mormon Wives' Star Jen Affleck Files for Divorce From Husband Zac While Pregnant

The 27-year-old reality star filed in a Utah court on Tuesday, four months after announcing her fourth pregnancy. The couple married in 2019 and have three children.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

"Secret Lives of Mormon Wives" star Jennifer Affleck is divorcing husband Zac Affleck after seven years of marriage.

The reality star, 27, filed a petition for divorce in a Utah court on Tuesday, November 3, according to records obtained by USA TODAY. She also submitted a proposed parenting plan for their children, Nora, 4, Luca, 3, and Penelope, 15 months. The filing does not publicly explain the reason for the separation or specify the final arrangements being sought.

Jennifer Affleck is currently pregnant with their fourth child. She announced the news in June, less than a year after welcoming their youngest. A source told People on Wednesday that Zac, 30, "is doing okay" following Jen's filing, and that the two are "staying focused on preparing for the baby and supporting their kids."

The pair met on the Mormon dating app Mutual in 2018 and married on June 28, 2019. Their relationship difficulties have played out on Hulu's reality series, including a period apart in the second season, when Jen discovered she was pregnant with their third child.

Jen is set to appear in the upcoming spinoff, "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County," premiering on Hulu on November 12. The series follows her move from Utah to Southern California.

Reporting: USA Today
