Ravens Down to Third-String QB as Jackson and Huntley Injuries Force Fagnano Start

Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano will start for Baltimore against Jacksonville on Thursday night, with Lamar Jackson headed to injured reserve and Tyler Huntley out for the season.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 4, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano will start for Baltimore against the Jacksonville Jaguars on Thursday night, according to Adam Schefter reporting cited on November 4.

Lamar Jackson is out indefinitely and headed to injured reserve with a thumb injury, and backup Tyler Huntley suffered a season-ending turf toe injury, according to the same reporting.

Baltimore signed Kedon Slovis off the Green Bay Packers' practice squad to the 53-man roster on Tuesday, November 4, to provide depth behind Fagnano.

The Ravens are reported to be exploring veteran quarterback trade options behind the scenes ahead of the November 10 trade deadline, according to Ian Rapoport reporting cited on November 3-4.

One November 4 roundup described Jackson's injury as an ankle injury, but the thumb and injured-reserve version is the one corroborated by multiple November 4 reports citing Schefter and Rapoport, and it is the version used here.
