SNL'S HALLOWEEN NIGHT: INDE NAVARRETTE HOSTS, GRACIE ABRAMS PERFORMS

Saturday Night Live returns from its two-week hiatus with a Halloween night episode on Saturday, hosted by Inde Navarrette with Gracie Abrams as musical guest.

ONOrbit News NetworkOct 31, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Saturday Night Live returns from its two-week hiatus with a Halloween night episode on Saturday, October 31, airing at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.

Actress Inde Navarrette, breakout star of the hit horror film 'Obsession,' makes her hosting debut in one of the few Halloween night episodes in the show's history.

Gracie Abrams returns as musical guest for her second appearance, following her debut on December 14, 2024, and following the release of her third studio album, 'Daughter from Hell.'

The episode follows comedian Shane Gillis's controversial episode, after which the show took a short break before returning for Season 52's Halloween show.

Halloween night episodes are rare for the long-running sketch show, making Saturday's edition one of the season's most anticipated.
