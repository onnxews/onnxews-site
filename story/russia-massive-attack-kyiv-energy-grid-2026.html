Russia Pounds Kyiv With 172 Drones and 36 Ballistic Missiles Days Before Peace Talks

The overnight assault hit power plants and a major bridge across the Dnipro, leaving the capital struggling for electricity, heat and water as temperatures neared freezing.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 7, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Russian forces launched a massive overnight missile and drone attack on Kyiv and the surrounding region, damaging energy infrastructure and a major bridge across the Dnipro River just days before planned peace talks between Ukraine, Russia and the United States.

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Saturday that Russia fired 172 drones and 36 ballistic missiles at Kyiv and several other regions overnight, with all of the missiles aimed at the capital and its surroundings. Ukraine's air force said its defences had shot down or suppressed four missiles and 149 drones by 9 a.m., including 137 jet-powered drones.

The attack came in three waves, beginning at about 11 p.m. on Friday and continuing through the early hours of Saturday. Several missiles struck the Northern Bridge, one of the capital's key crossings over the Dnipro, damaging the roadway, the trolleybus lines and a power line. The bridge was temporarily closed in both directions.

One person was killed and eight people, including a child, were wounded in the overnight attack, according to authorities. Energy facilities in at least three districts of the Kyiv region were hit, and Mayor Vitali Klitschko described an extremely difficult situation for power, heating and water supplies in the capital, where temperatures hovered near freezing on Saturday morning.

Zelenskyy said a substation connecting the Rivne and Khmelnytskyi nuclear power plants to Kyiv and Ukraine's central regions was among the targets hit, along with substations in the Vinnytsya, Zhytomyr, Rivne, Kharkiv and Dnipropetrovsk regions. Elsewhere, strikes on four districts of the Dnipropetrovsk region killed one person and injured four others, including an 11-year-old girl.

Moscow's defence ministry said it had carried out a massive strike on targets in Ukraine's military-industrial sector as well as energy and transport infrastructure in and around Kyiv, and claimed the bridge was used to transport weapons and military equipment. Russia has stepped up air attacks on Ukrainian cities as its ground offensive has largely stalled, pounding energy infrastructure with difficult-to-intercept missiles and drones more than four and a half years into its full-scale invasion.

Ukraine struck back overnight. Its general staff said missile forces used Neptune coastal missiles to hit a military-industrial plant in Oryol, Russia, that produces electronic components, and Ukrainian forces struck two Iskander missile launchers and a Pantsir air-defence system in Russia's Bryansk region.

The timing of the assault was pointed. Zelenskyy said Russia was preparing for trilateral talks by launching ballistic missiles at Ukraine's critical infrastructure. The talks, involving Ukraine, Russia and the United States, are scheduled for November 10 and 11 in Abu Dhabi, with de-escalation, prisoner releases and the format of a possible three-way summit on the agenda.

Repair crews were working across the affected regions on Saturday wherever the security situation allowed. For Kyiv's residents, the attack was a reminder of the winter ahead: last winter temperatures in parts of Ukraine dropped to minus 21 degrees Celsius, and international monitors have noted that attacks on power plants have intensified once again. Whether the Abu Dhabi talks can produce even a pause in that campaign will be the test of the coming week.
