PSG Set Champions League Record in 6-0 Rout of Villarreal

Paris Saint-Germain scored four goals inside the first 17 minutes and stayed perfect in the league phase, top with 12 points from four wins.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 4, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Paris Saint-Germain won 6-0 away at Villarreal on Tuesday, November 3, in UEFA Champions League league-phase Matchday 4, Reuters reported.

PSG set a record as the fastest team to score four goals in a Champions League match on their way to the 6-0 win. Roundups report the four goals came inside the first 17 minutes, clocked at 16 minutes and 2 seconds to the fourth goal, from Joao Neves, Desire Doue, Senny Mayulu and Fabian Ruiz.

The rout was effectively over before the midway point of the first half, and PSG cruised through the second half to complete the six-goal margin.

PSG remain top of the league-phase standings with 12 points from four wins in four matches, followed by a group of six teams on nine points.

Elsewhere on the same night, Barcelona beat Aston Villa 3-0 and Bayern Munich beat Atletico Madrid 2-0.

Reporting: Reuters
