'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' Hits $2.52 Billion, Third-Highest Gross Ever

"Spider-Man: Brand New Day" grossed $2.52 billion worldwide, the third-highest total ever, driving Sony Pictures sales up 37 percent in the quarter.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

Sony Pictures Entertainment reported sales of $3.23 billion for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, up 37 percent year over year, powered by the summer run of "Spider-Man: Brand New Day."

Theatrical revenue came in at about $1.28 billion, roughly 11 times the prior-year figure. Across all windows, including theatrical, home entertainment and TV and streaming licensing, motion pictures revenue grew 155 percent to $1.82 billion.

"Spider-Man: Brand New Day" grossed $2.52 billion at the global box office this summer. That makes it the third-highest-grossing film in history, behind only "Avatar" and "Avengers: Endgame," before adjusting for inflation, and Sony Pictures' biggest film to date.

The movie opened to a record-setting $360 million domestic weekend after its July 31 wide release. Supporting releases added to the total, including "Resident Evil," a September release that has grossed about $400 million worldwide.

TV productions revenue was $483 million, down 31 percent from $701 million. The next big Sony Pictures release is "Jumanji: Open World" on December 25. The next quarter looks tougher: two fall releases, "The Social Reckoning" and "Klara and the Sun," have already underperformed at the box office.
