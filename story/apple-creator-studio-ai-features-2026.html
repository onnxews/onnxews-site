Apple Rolls Out Creator Studio AI Features Powered by Apple Intelligence and OpenAI

Final Cut Pro gains built-in MotionVFX, Logic Pro gets a new AI assistant, and Keynote, Pages and Numbers add AI helpers that understand document context.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Apple has begun rolling out the new AI features for its Creator Studio apps that it announced late last month, built on a combination of Apple Intelligence and OpenAI models. The updates are free for existing subscribers.

Final Cut Pro for Mac now integrates MotionVFX directly into the app, giving editors access to thousands of templates, transitions, titles and motion graphics, along with tools for rotoscoping, tracking and color grading. Editors can also tap into Apple's curated Content Hub of stock videos, photos and backgrounds without leaving the app.

Music creators using Logic Pro for Mac gain a new intelligent assistant in beta, powered by a combination of Apple-built models and OpenAI, that helps navigate production workflows, start a new song or set up a vocal track from a single prompt. An enhanced Stem Splitter now separates drums as well.

Keynote, Pages and Numbers introduce new AI features in beta, including assistants that understand document context and new Model Context Protocol support for workflow automation. In Keynote, the assistant helps design and animate slides; in Pages, it drafts and restructures written content; in Numbers, it calculates, visualizes and organizes data and recommends chart types.

Pixelmator Pro introduces Generate Fill, which lets creators transform selected areas of an image using natural language. Apple announced the updates alongside the new iMacs and 14-inch MacBook Pro powered by the M6 chip.
