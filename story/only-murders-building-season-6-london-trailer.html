Only Murders in the Building Heads to London in Season 6 Trailer

Hulu's hit mystery moves to London for its sixth season, with Tina Fey's Cinda Canning as the victim and Nicola Coughlan as the prime suspect.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 18, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Hulu dropped the official trailer for the sixth season of Only Murders in the Building on Tuesday, confirming that the hit comedy-mystery is moving to London — and that the victim this time is one of the show's own. The season, retitled "Only Murders in London," premieres on Tuesday, December 8, 2026 on Hulu in the United States and Disney+ internationally.

The first two episodes will arrive at launch, with new episodes following weekly on Tuesdays and the finale scheduled for Tuesday, January 26, 2027. The season runs to 10 episodes. The trailer picks up where season five ended: true-crime podcaster Cinda Canning, played by Tina Fey, is found murdered on the doorstep of the Arconia, wearing ginger curls, and the trio of Charles, Oliver and Mabel must follow her to London to find her killer.

The official synopsis sets out the premise. "After successfully bringing the guilty to justice once again, Charles, Oliver and Mabel embark on their hardest case yet when the next victim — Cinda Canning — arrives at their doorstep, summoning them to London to catch her killer." In the trailer, Mabel, played by Selena Gomez, puts it more plainly: "Cinda died dropping a hand on our gate, she clearly wanted our help." Detective Williams, played by Da'Vine Joy Randolph, tells the trio that Cinda had been working in the United Kingdom.

The London move brings a new suspect into focus. Nicola Coughlan joins the cast as Gemma Joyce, described as "the girl with the curls," a woman suspected of murdering a royal, whom the trio visit in prison in the trailer. Cinda's final podcast was called "The Girl With the Curls," and the trailer suggests the two women are connected in ways the trio will have to untangle.

The guest cast is the season's biggest draw. Martin Freeman plays the British version of Brazzos, the television detective Charles once played, and the trailer has him dismissing Charles as a "talentless hack," prompting Mabel's reply: "I hate this guy; no one makes fun of our Charles except us." The season also features Olivia Colman, David Tennant, Peter Capaldi and Jodie Whittaker — three actors who have each played the Doctor in Doctor Who — alongside Jim Broadbent, Jennifer Saunders, Simone Ashley, Sharon Horgan, Richard E. Grant, Geri Halliwell-Horner, Lesley Nicol, Jane Horrocks, Richard Ayoade, Matthew Beard, Rhea Norwood, Sean Teale, Anjana Vasan, Jamie Demetriou and Meryl Streep.

Series creator John Hoffman told Decider at the 2026 Emmy Awards that the Doctor Who casting "was a bit of a coincidence. It wasn't intended, but I mean, how do you not pick as many doctors as you can?" The line captures the season's approach: the London setting is a chance to gather the cream of British acting talent around the three leads, played by Steve Martin, Martin Short and Selena Gomez, whose chemistry has carried the show through five seasons.

The trailer ends on a cliffhanger. Oliver, played by Martin Short, finds a diamond headband with blood on it just as security arrives, leaving the trio — and the audience — to wonder whose it is and what it means. It is the kind of closing image the show has used to set its mysteries in motion since the first season, when a murder in the Arconia turned three neighbors into amateur detectives.

The season is the show's first outside New York, and the move is a risk as well as an opportunity. The Arconia, the Upper West Side apartment building at the heart of the series, has been as much a character as any of the leads, and the show will have to prove it can keep its tone — part cozy mystery, part show-business satire — in a new city. Parade and Decider both covered the trailer on Tuesday. Hulu's basic plan with ads costs $11.99 a month, with an ad-free plan at $18.99 a month.

Why it matters: Moving Only Murders to London is the show's boldest creative bet since it began, and the trailer shows why it might work. The premise — a murdered podcaster summoning the trio to her own city — gives the season a built-in reason to leave the Arconia, and the guest cast is a roll call of British acting royalty that no American network show could assemble. The risk is real: the show's charm has always been inseparable from its New York building and its three leads' chemistry, and a season built on cameos could tip into stunt casting. But with Martin Freeman playing a British Brazzos and three former Doctors in the mix, the show is betting that its audience will follow it anywhere — and the trailer is designed to make that bet look safe.
