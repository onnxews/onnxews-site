Imran Khan Health Claims Disputed as PTI Demands Hospital Transfer

The party says the jailed former prime minister's blood pressure reached 192 and wants him moved to Shifa International Hospital. His own lawyer says he was not the source of the alarming reports.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

The source of alarming claims about former Pakistani Prime Minister Imran Khan's health remains unclear, particularly after his lawyer distanced himself from being the source of the information.

Khan's sister Rubina Khan, in a statement shared on social media by his son Kasim Khan, claimed her brother had conveyed a message that his blood pressure had suddenly risen to 192, alleging that his life was under threat in jail. She demanded he be immediately admitted to an independent medical institution in the presence of his personal doctor.

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf has demanded Khan's immediate transfer to Shifa International Hospital in Islamabad for treatment by his personal doctors, according to party statements. The party says the reported reading followed a meeting on November 2 at Adiala jail in Rawalpindi between Khan and his lawyer, Salman Safdar, the first such lawyer meeting since April.

However, when approached about the reports, Safdar said he was not the source of what was being said about Khan's health. Sources familiar with the circumstances surrounding the meeting offered a somewhat different account: Khan did mention that his blood pressure had once risen to around 190, but they described him as otherwise generally fit and did not indicate any immediate serious medical emergency.

The family says a Supreme Court order from August requiring treatment at Shifa International Hospital has not been carried out. The government has not published its own medical account of the reported episode. The figure of 192 should be treated as a claim by the party and Khan's family, not as a confirmed medical finding. The Islamabad High Court was due on Thursday to hear a petition seeking permission to meet Khan.
