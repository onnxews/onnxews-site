Jaguars Rout Injury-Hit Ravens 33-10 as Fagnano's First Start Falls Flat

Trevor Lawrence threw for 242 yards and Cam Little kicked four field goals as Jacksonville improved to 6-2, while Baltimore lost its fourth straight behind third-string quarterback Joe Fagnano.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

The Jacksonville Jaguars sailed to a 33-10 victory over the host Baltimore Ravens on a rainy Thursday night, taking advantage of a Baltimore team down to its third-string quarterback.

Trevor Lawrence passed for 242 yards and one touchdown, completing 16 of 26 passes, and Cam Little kicked four field goals. Bhayshul Tuten and Chris Rodriguez Jr. rushed for touchdowns, and Parker Washington caught a 50-yard touchdown pass just before halftime that made it 17-3.

Jacksonville's defense held the Ravens to 227 total yards and forced three turnovers, with interceptions from Montaric Brown and Jourdan Lewis and a fumble recovery by Jalen McLeod. The Jaguars, now 6-2, have won five of their past six games.

Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano made his first career NFL start for Baltimore with Lamar Jackson sidelined by a right thumb injury and Tyler Huntley out with a right toe injury. Fagnano completed 10 of 18 passes for 60 yards and two interceptions. Baltimore's lone touchdown came on a 65-yard run by Justice Hill in the fourth quarter.

Derrick Henry rushed for 57 yards on 17 carries and moved into seventh place on the NFL's all-time rushing list with 13,707 yards, passing Jerome Bettis and LaDainian Tomlinson. Baltimore, now 4-5, has lost four straight games and has been outscored 84-10 in the second half during the slide.
