NBA Roundup: George Scores 36 as Celtics Thump Sixers in Reunion Night

Paul George torched his former team for 36 points as Boston took the East's best record, while the Knicks, Heat, Blazers, Suns and Magic all won.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Paul George scored 36 points against his former team to highlight the Boston Celtics' 123-101 road victory over the Philadelphia 76ers on Tuesday night, in a game loaded with reunions, revenge and the best record in the Eastern Conference. Boston made 22 of 49 three-point attempts, shooting 44.9 percent from deep, and improved to 9-2, just ahead of the 76ers at 9-3.

George was the story from the opening minutes. Facing the team he played for over the past two seasons, he shot 14 of 22 from the field and sank six of his 11 attempts from three-point range, adding five rebounds as the Celtics opened up a 16-point lead and never let Philadelphia back into it. The crowd in Philadelphia, which had welcomed him back with mixed feelings, watched him dismantle his old team.

The game also featured Jaylen Brown against Jayson Tatum for the first time since the trade that sent Brown to Philadelphia, and the return of George to Philadelphia after two years with the Sixers. Brown finished with 13 points and five rebounds for the 76ers, while Tatum and the Celtics kept their composure in a game that carried plenty of emotion on both sides.

Tyrese Maxey led Philadelphia with 24 points and four assists, while Joel Embiid added 16 points and six rebounds. LeBron James, now a Sixer, had 11 points, eight rebounds and five assists, and VJ Edgecombe added eight points, five rebounds and four assists. It was not enough against a Boston team that shot the lights out and controlled the game from the second quarter on.

The night's other games had their own headlines. The New York Knicks rode dominant second- and fourth-quarter runs to a 117-85 win over the Milwaukee Bucks at Madison Square Garden. Karl-Anthony Towns continued his season-opening streak of double-doubles, and Jalen Brunson scored 23 points to reach 11,000 points for his career.

In Salt Lake City, the Miami Heat beat the Utah Jazz 115-109 behind 30 points and 11 rebounds from Giannis Antetokounmpo, who made 12 of his 21 shots from the field and added four assists with just one turnover in 31 minutes. The win kept Miami's strong start going, while the Jazz fell to their ninth straight loss.

Portland handed Oklahoma City a 123-103 defeat at the Moda Center, and the night belonged to Damian Lillard, who became the Trail Blazers' career leader in field goals attempted. It was a milestone for a player whose name is already all over the franchise record book, and it came in a comfortable win over one of the West's best teams.

The Phoenix Suns routed the LA Clippers 133-110, seizing control in the first quarter and never letting go. Jordan Goodwin paced the onslaught by shooting 6 of 7 from the field and hitting all six of his three-point attempts to finish with 18 points, and the Suns led by as many as 32 points in the third quarter. The Clippers fell to 2-8.

The Orlando Magic beat the Sacramento Kings 107-100 behind a game-high 33 points from Franz Wagner, who went 11 of 23 from the floor and a perfect 10 of 10 from the free-throw line, adding seven assists, seven rebounds and two steals in 38 minutes. The Kings fell to 1-10, one of the worst starts in the league.

Tuesday's results sharpened the early-season picture. Boston owns the East's best record at 9-2, with Philadelphia right behind at 9-3, and the two teams look set to spend the season trading punches at the top. The Knicks have now won four straight, the Magic are finding their footing, and the Kings and Clippers are searching for answers. With the season still young, the standings will shift again this week, but the Celtics sent a message in Philadelphia: the road to the East runs through Boston.
