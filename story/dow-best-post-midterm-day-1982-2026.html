Dow Posts Best Post-Midterm Day in 44 Years as Democrats Take Congress

The Dow jumped 1,569 points in its best day since April 2025, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq also rising sharply, as traders bet divided government will cool the Iran war and policy whiplash.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 5, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

U.S. stocks soared on Wednesday as Wall Street digested the resounding Democratic sweep in the midterm elections and mulled what a divided government will mean for the war with Iran, President Donald Trump's policy agenda and the AI trade.

The Dow surged 1,569 points, or 3 percent, to close at 53,356.12, and had its best day since April 2025. The Dow also posted its biggest post-midterm elections single-day gain since 1982, according to data from CFRA Research. It moved back above 53,000 for the first time since September 8.

The S&P 500 rose 1.58 percent to 7,688.62, its strongest day in three months, and the Nasdaq Composite added 1.44 percent to 25,736.51. The VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, sank almost 20 percent to about 16.6.

Analysts said investors read divided government as a brake on sharp policy shifts and on the Iran war, which has driven oil prices higher. "Investors could conclude that Washington remains structurally constrained, limiting the prospects for dramatic shifts in the economic outlook," Michael Krautzberger, an analyst at Allianz Global Investors, told Reuters.

The rally was broad, with consumer discretionary, financials and real estate leading, but data-center names fell on fears of tighter AI regulation. Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 0.7 percent early Thursday as the chip selloff continued overnight.
