Trump Offers Dana White the Press Secretary Job. White Says He Will Meet Him Monday

The president announced on Truth Social that he had offered the White House podium to the UFC chief — then a source told CNN the offer had been declined, and an aide said Trump was joking.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 8, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

President Donald Trump announced on Saturday that he has offered the job of White House press secretary to Dana White, the chief executive of the Ultimate Fighting Championship, in an announcement that was unconventional even by the standards of a norm-busting presidency. White said on Saturday that he plans to meet with Trump on Monday at the White House.

Trump made the offer in a post on Truth Social while spending the day at his golf club in Florida. He wrote that he was pleased to announce that the great Dana White, a violent fighter and warrior, had been offered the job of press secretary of the United States, and he urged White to take the role.

The president went further, saying he had requested that White bring along several UFC fighters as assistants, even though some are not from the United States. He named Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes and Kayla Harrison, and said the group was needed to defend the administration against the lunatic left. He closed the post by writing that White would be a press secretary like none other, and adding: Dana, please take the job.

White, who was in London on Saturday for a boxing event, was asked about the offer at a news conference and responded by saying he planned to fly to Washington right away and meet with Trump on Monday, though he declined to get into specifics. He also posted a photograph of himself standing behind the press secretary's lectern in the White House, captioned: On to the next chapter.

Whether the offer was serious remains unclear. CNN reported, citing a source familiar with the matter, that White had declined the idea, while one White House aide told the network that Trump was joking about the offer. White House communications director Steven Cheung reposted the president's message, adding: Hell yeah! Do it, @DanaWhite! The White House did not respond to requests for comment.

The press secretary role has been open since Katie Zacharia left the job after only five days in October, the shortest stint in the position's history. Trump fired Zacharia after one rocky briefing. She had succeeded Karoline Leavitt, who resigned from the position in August. Trump has struggled to find someone to fill a post he is personally invested in, and the search has now produced one of the most unusual episodes of his presidency.

White is a longtime friend, supporter and donor to Trump, and the two men have appeared together frequently at UFC events. The mixed martial arts promotion is a major business in its own right: its parent company, TKO Holdings, reported that the UFC had revenue of 519.2 million dollars in the third quarter of 2026.

The episode comes at a delicate moment for the administration, days after Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress in the midterm elections and the day after Trump promoted Stephen Miller to White House chief of staff. The White House is in transition, with a new chief of staff taking over on Monday and the communications operation still without a permanent public face.

What happens on Monday may settle the question of whether the offer was a joke, a trial balloon or a genuine attempt to install one of the president's most loyal allies behind the briefing room podium. White has said only that he will meet Trump. For a White House that has cycled through press secretaries at a remarkable pace, the meeting will be watched closely — and, whatever its outcome, the search for a spokesperson continues.
