John Mulaney Hosts "SNL" for a Seventh Time With Phoebe Bridgers as Musical Guest

The comedian's seventh hosting turn airs Saturday at 11:30 p.m. ET, with Phoebe Bridgers performing songs from her August album "Lost Weekend."

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 14, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

John Mulaney returns to "Saturday Night Live" on Saturday, hosting the show for the seventh time, with Phoebe Bridgers as the musical guest.

The episode is the sixth of Season 52 and airs at 11:30 p.m. Eastern on NBC, streaming live on Peacock and available on Peacock the next day. It is Mulaney's first time hosting since November 2024.

Mulaney's hosting history with the show goes back to April 14, 2018. He returned on March 2, 2019, February 29, 2020, October 31, 2020, February 26, 2022, and November 2, 2024. With seven hosting gigs, he is the most prolific host of the past 10 years.

His connection to the show runs deeper than the hosting chair. Mulaney was a writer on "Saturday Night Live" from 2008 to 2013, where he co-created the Stefon character with Bill Hader. He joined the show's Five-Timers Club in 2022, and his hosting appearances have become a fixture of the show's calendar.

Mulaney is currently on his "Mister Whatever" stand-up tour, which runs through March 2027. The tour has kept him on the road for much of the year, and the "Saturday Night Live" appearance is a break from the road that brings him back to the studio where he started.

Phoebe Bridgers joins him as musical guest. Her most recent album, "Lost Weekend," was released in August 2026. It is her third appearance on the show overall and her second as a solo musical guest, after performing once with Boygenius.

The season began on September 26 with Jalen Brunson hosting and KATSEYE as musical guest. Since then, the hosts have included Dakota Johnson, Shane Gillis and Inde Navarrette on October 31, followed by Druski on November 7. Druski's episode drew the show's highest ratings in two years, according to NBC, setting a high bar for Mulaney's return.

Mulaney's episodes are known for their ambition, from musical sketches to elaborate set pieces, and his return is one of the most anticipated of the season. With Bridgers providing the music and Mulaney's track record behind him, Saturday's show has the makings of a standout night.

Mulaney's relationship with the show is unusual. Most hosts are actors or musicians promoting a project; Mulaney is a former writer returning to the building where he learned his craft. That history shows in his episodes, which tend to be packed with inside jokes and elaborate sketches that play to the show's strengths rather than to a guest's latest release.

The show's season has been building momentum. The September premiere drew attention, the October episodes held it, and Druski's November 7 show delivered the best ratings in two years. Mulaney's return is the kind of booking that can keep that run going, and if the episode lands, it will confirm that the season's strategy of mixing new faces with returning favorites is working.

For viewers, the episode is also a reminder of how the show builds its seasons around returning favorites. Mulaney is not a one-off guest but a part of the show's fabric, and his seventh hosting turn is a milestone few comedians reach. Read the preview from NBC

Why it matters: Mulaney's seventh hosting gig cements his status as the show's favorite returning host of the past decade, and it comes at a good moment for "Saturday Night Live," fresh off Druski's ratings high last week. The pairing with Bridgers, whose album arrived in August, gives the episode a current musical hook to match the comedy. If the show can keep the momentum going, the season is shaping up as one of its strongest in years, built on hosts who know the building.
