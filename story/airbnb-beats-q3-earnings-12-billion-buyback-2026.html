Airbnb Beats Earnings and Unveils $12 Billion Buyback as Travel Demand Surges

The travel platform's summer quarter grew 19 percent, its fastest in more than three years, and it raised its full-year outlook.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Airbnb reported a strong summer quarter on Thursday, beating Wall Street's expectations on both profit and revenue and raising its outlook for the full year. The company earned $3.16 per share, well above the $2.86 analysts had expected and up from $2.21 a year earlier, an earnings surprise of more than 10 percent.

Revenue rose 19 percent from a year earlier to $4.87 billion, the company's fastest growth in more than three years, beating the consensus estimate of about $4.75 billion. Gross booking value climbed 21 percent to $27.7 billion, and nights and seats booked rose 16 percent to 155 million, with every region growing and North America posting its fastest growth in four years. Net income jumped 38 percent to $1.89 billion.

Airbnb also authorized a new share buyback of up to $12 billion, a sign of confidence after the strong quarter. Shares jumped more than 10 percent in after-hours trading to about $188.67, after closing down 3.5 percent in the regular session on a day the Nasdaq fell 5.5 percent.

For the final three months of the year, Airbnb forecast revenue of $3.22 billion to $3.28 billion, up 16 to 18 percent from a year earlier. For the full year, the company now expects revenue growth in the high teens, up from the mid-teens growth it projected in August.

The results stand out in a week when many technology and travel stocks were under pressure. Airbnb shares have gained about 30 percent since the start of the year, compared with a 12.3 percent rise for the S&P 500, though the company kept a slightly smaller share of each booking as revenue and its free cash flow grew far more slowly than its profit.
