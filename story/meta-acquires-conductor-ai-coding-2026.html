Meta Acquires AI Coding Startup Conductor as the Race for Multi-Agent Tools Heats Up

Conductor lets developers run several AI coding agents at once in parallel workspaces. Its team will join Meta's Superintelligence Labs, and its cloud service shuts down November 20.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Meta has acquired Conductor, an AI coding startup that lets developers spin up software projects by deploying multiple coding agents into parallel workspaces. Meta confirmed the acquisition to TechCrunch but did not disclose financial terms or provide further details.

Conductor was founded in 2024 and only launched its core product last summer. Its platform is designed to help software engineers work on separate, compartmentalized parts of a project at the same time, with each agent working in its own workspace. The agents it deploys are not built in-house but come from major labs such as Anthropic and OpenAI.

Founder and chief executive Charlie Holtz announced the deal on Wednesday, writing, "I have big news: Conductor is joining Meta." He said the company has "something new" planned for customers by early next year, and said he looks forward to working with Meta chief AI officer Alexandr Wang and Nat Friedman, head of product at Meta Superintelligence Labs.

For existing Conductor users, the transition comes with a firm deadline. New sign-ups and downloads have already stopped, and the company's cloud service will shut down on November 20, after which it will begin deleting user cloud data. Core features of the local app will keep working until early next year.

The deal is Meta's latest move to catch up in AI coding tools. The company launched its own coding agent, Muse Code, in August, and recently said its Muse agents have passed 10 million users.

Reporting: TechCrunch
