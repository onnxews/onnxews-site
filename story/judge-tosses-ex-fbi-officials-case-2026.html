Judge Tosses Case Against Ex-FBI Officials Over Hunter Biden Probes

A federal judge on Monday dismissed the criminal case accusing two former senior FBI officials of lying about their involvement in investigations into Hunter Biden.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 3, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

A federal judge on Monday tossed out a criminal case accusing two former senior FBI officials of lying about their involvement in investigations into Hunter Biden, son of former President Joe Biden, Reuters reported. The dismissal ended the prosecution before trial.

The case had accused the former officials of making false statements about their roles in the politically charged investigations. Further detail on the judge's reasoning was not included in the initial reporting, and the article does not state a reason beyond the dismissal itself.

The Hunter Biden investigations have been a fault line in American politics for years, with Republicans accusing federal law enforcement of slow-walking or shielding the younger Biden, and Democrats accusing investigators of pursuing him for political reasons.

The dismissal is a setback for prosecutors who brought the case, though it was not immediately clear whether the government would appeal or seek to refile. Dismissals on procedural or evidentiary grounds do not necessarily resolve the underlying factual disputes.

For the former officials, the ruling removes the prospect of a criminal trial. For the broader debate over the bureau's handling of the Hunter Biden matters, it is likely to be cited by both sides — by critics of the prosecution as vindication, and by its defenders as a legal ruling, not an exoneration on the facts.

Reporting: Reuters
