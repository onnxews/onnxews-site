HEIDIWEEN IS COMING — AND IT'S BLOODY: Heidi Klum Teases Her 2026 Halloween Costume

The queen of Halloween teased her 2026 costume one week before her annual NYC party — a close-up of a grotesque prosthetic — and warned fans this year's look is “very bloody.”

By Peter Osibajo · Orbit News NetworkOct 26, 2026 · 3 min readShare on FacebookShare on X

NEW YORK — Heidi Klum, the queen of Halloween, just teased her 2026 costume one week before her annual New York City party — a close-up of a grotesque prosthetic, with one question for fans: “What's my costume?”

She's already warned fans this year's look is “very bloody” and “goes very well with red wine.” After Medusa last year, the bar is high — and Klum has made a career of clearing it.

Her annual party has become Halloween's main event, with each year's reveal dissected for days. One week to go.
