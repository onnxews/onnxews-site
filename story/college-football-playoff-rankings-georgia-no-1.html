Georgia Takes No. 1 in New College Football Playoff Rankings as Miami Tumbles to Seventh

The Bulldogs rose to No. 1 after a 42-7 win at Ole Miss, while Miami fell six spots after losing to Notre Dame.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

Georgia is the new No. 1 team in the College Football Playoff rankings. The selection committee released its second Top 25 of the 2026 season on Tuesday night, November 10, elevating the Bulldogs one spot after their 42-7 road win over Ole Miss, and the new rankings shook up the playoff picture with four weeks left in the regular season.

Georgia, which is 8-1 overall and 6-1 in the SEC, moved to No. 1 in the Associated Press Top 25 on Sunday as well, and the committee agreed. The Bulldogs return to Sanford Stadium to face Missouri on Saturday, November 14, at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC, with a chance to strengthen their hold on the top spot.

Utah follows Georgia at No. 2, with the Utes unbeaten at 9-0, and Alabama sits at No. 3 at 7-2 after a 31-13 win over LSU. UCLA climbed four spots to No. 4, also unbeaten at 9-0, passing Miami, Texas and Kentucky in the process. Notre Dame rounds out the top five at 8-1, up six spots after its statement win over the weekend.

The biggest fall belonged to Miami. The Hurricanes entered the week as the No. 1 team in the initial rankings, but dropped to No. 7 after a 31-24 loss to Notre Dame on Saturday. The drop drew immediate criticism, with some analysts calling the committee's treatment of Miami 'ridiculous,' arguing that a one-loss team that had been ranked first should not fall six spots for losing to a top-five team on the road.

Notre Dame was the biggest winner of the week. The Fighting Irish moved up from No. 11 to No. 5 on the strength of the Miami win, putting them firmly in the playoff field in the committee's projection. Tennessee jumped five spots to No. 8, and Oregon was the biggest climber in the rankings, moving up seven spots to No. 16 after its comeback win over Ohio State.

The falls were just as dramatic further down. Ohio State dropped to No. 20 at 6-3, out of the playoff picture for now, after losing to Oregon. LSU fell to No. 21 from No. 14 after its loss to Alabama, although the Tigers still have a path back if they can beat Texas, Tennessee and Arkansas to finish the season. Two teams fell out of the top 12 entirely in the space of one week.

The full top 10 reads: Georgia, Utah, Alabama, UCLA, Notre Dame, Texas, Miami, Tennessee, Indiana and BYU. UCLA is the highest-ranked Big Ten team, and in the committee's projected bracket the Bruins would earn a first-round bye and face the winner of Notre Dame and Boise State. Georgia, Utah, Alabama and UCLA would all earn first-round byes as the top four teams.

Boise State, ranked No. 24 at 8-1, continues to lead the teams from the Group of Six conferences. The highest-ranked champion from those six leagues is guaranteed a berth in the playoff, and in the current projection Boise State would be the lone Group of Six representative, earning the No. 12 seed and a first-round trip to face No. 5 Notre Dame.

The 2026 playoff remains a 12-team field. The champions of the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC receive automatic bids, along with the highest-ranked champion from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt. In the committee's second projection, the SEC would have five teams in the field, the Big Ten and Big 12 would have two each, the ACC would have one, and Notre Dame would make it as an independent.

The rankings are a snapshot, and the committee's own chair, Hunter Yurachek, would be the first to say they are destined to change. Three weeks of the regular season remain, plus conference championship games, and the final rankings and playoff bracket will be released on Sunday, December 6. The first round is set for December 18 and 19, with the national championship on January 25. For now, Georgia sits on top, Miami is left to argue its case, and Ohio State is left to climb.
