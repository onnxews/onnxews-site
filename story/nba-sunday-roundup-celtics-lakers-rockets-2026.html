Tatum Scores 32 as Celtics Rout Mavericks; Lakers and Rockets Roll on Sunday

Jayson Tatum scored 32 points as the Boston Celtics blew out the Dallas Mavericks 131-94, Luka Doncic poured in 43 as the Los Angeles Lakers held off the Phoenix Suns 131-123, and the Houston Rockets routed the Washington Wizards 147-119 on Sunday night.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 16, 2026 · 4 min readFacebookX

The Boston Celtics delivered the most emphatic performance of Sunday's NBA slate, routing the Dallas Mavericks 131-94 at TD Garden to extend their winning streak to six games. The Celtics led 42-9 after the first quarter, a 33-point advantage that stands as the largest lead after one quarter in franchise history, and they never trailed on the way to a 37-point victory.

Jayson Tatum led the way with 32 points and six assists, shooting 11 of 18 from the field. Tatum scored 21 of his points in the third quarter and sat out the entire fourth with the game well in hand, as did the rest of Boston's starters. Derrick White added 16 points, including four three-pointers, and reserve Baylor Scheierman chipped in 12.

Boston shot 51 percent from the field and made 23 of 55 three-point attempts, building a lead that reached as many as 41 points. The Celtics improved to 11-2 and stayed perfect at home at 6-0, and they will look for a seventh straight win when they host the Orlando Magic on Monday night. Dallas had no answer for Boston's start. Backup Zaccharie Risacher led the Mavericks with 18 points and five rebounds, while Cooper Flagg finished with 17 points, four assists and three rebounds. The Mavericks, who have now lost three straight games, were without Kyrie Irving because of left knee injury management and fell to 0-3 this season when he does not play.

The Los Angeles Lakers also kept rolling on Sunday, holding off the Phoenix Suns 131-123 at Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix to stretch their winning streak to eight games. Luka Doncic scored 43 points with five three-pointers, Quentin Grimes added 22 points, and Walker Kessler posted 14 points and 12 rebounds for his eighth double-double, with Collin Sexton adding 16 points off the bench.

Los Angeles scored the first 10 points of the game and led by as many as 31 in a wire-to-wire victory. Phoenix made it interesting late behind reserve Collin Gillespie, who scored 26 points, and Luke Kennard, who added 18, while Devin Booker finished with 17 points. The Suns finished 4-3 on a season-long seven-game homestand.

The Houston Rockets completed the night's trio of blowouts by trouncing the Washington Wizards 147-119 at Capital One Arena for their fourth straight win. Amen Thompson scored 26 points, Kevin Durant and Tari Eason added 25 each, and Alperen Sengun contributed 19 points as Houston dominated the middle two quarters 83-57 and shot 57.8 percent from the field, dishing out 40 assists.

Houston's starters barely had to work in the fourth quarter, with Thompson, Durant and Sengun all sitting out the entire final period. Alex Sarr led Washington with 25 points and nine rebounds, and rookie AJ Dybantsa added 16 points, but the Wizards skidded to their seventh consecutive loss.

The most striking line of the night in Washington belonged to Trae Young, who was held scoreless for the first time in his 512-game NBA career, missing all five of his field-goal attempts. The Rockets' defense smothered the Wizards' star guard, and Houston has now won four straight games.

Why it matters: Sunday's results sharpen the early-season picture in both conferences. Boston, at 11-2 with six straight wins and an unbeaten home record, looks every bit the championship contender, while the Lakers, riding eight consecutive victories behind Doncic's MVP-level scoring, look every bit the contender too. Houston's fourth straight win keeps the Rockets firmly in the Western Conference race, while Dallas and Washington are heading in the opposite direction: the Mavericks have lost three straight and struggle badly without Irving, and the Wizards' seventh consecutive defeat, capped by Young's first scoreless game, underlines how far they have to climb.
