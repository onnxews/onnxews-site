NHL Roundup: Jets Fans Boo Hellebuyck as Winnipeg Downs Devils

Dawson Mercer scored twice against his former team as the Jets beat the Devils 4-2 in Connor Hellebuyck's hostile return to Winnipeg.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

Dawson Mercer scored twice against his former team, including the tiebreaking power-play goal with 8:02 remaining in the third period, and the host Winnipeg Jets recorded a 4-2 victory over the New Jersey Devils on Tuesday night in one of the most emotionally charged games of the NHL season. The Jets won their fourth straight game following a nine-game skid, and they did it in front of fans who spent the night booing the man who used to be their hero.

That man is Connor Hellebuyck, the star goaltender who left Winnipeg for New Jersey and returned to his old rink for the first time as a Devil. Jets fans greeted him with derisive chants throughout the night, and the atmosphere was, by every account, hostile. Mercer, who was traded the other way, made the night his own, scoring twice against the team that drafted him, including the goal that broke a 2-2 tie late in the third.

The result continues a remarkable turnaround for Winnipeg. After losing nine straight games, the Jets have now won four in a row, and the victory over a Devils team that arrived at 13-6-0 is their most impressive of the streak. The Jets improved to 8-8-3, back to .500 and back in the playoff conversation after a start that had looked like a lost season.

The night's other games were just as dramatic. Kirill Kaprizov scored a power-play goal 18 seconds into overtime to give the visiting Minnesota Wild a 2-1 victory over the Montreal Canadiens, on a night when Montreal honored goaltending legend Carey Price. The Wild improved to 13-3-4, one of the best records in the league, while the Canadiens fell to 11-3-4.

The Washington Capitals edged the Ottawa Senators 3-2 in overtime, with the winner coming just over a minute into the extra period. The Capitals improved to 10-6-0, while the Senators fell to 11-6-2 in a game between two teams that have both started the season strongly.

The Colorado Avalanche beat the Toronto Maple Leafs 6-3 in Toronto, with Gabriel Landeskog scoring the game-winner early in the third period. The Avalanche improved to 11-6-2, while the Maple Leafs fell to 8-10-2, continuing a difficult start for a Toronto team that was expected to contend.

In Los Angeles, the Nashville Predators shut out the Kings 3-0 behind a 28-save performance from Juuse Saros and a two-goal night from Filip Forsberg. Forsberg opened the scoring in the first period and struck again in the second, and Adam Wilsby rounded out the scoring with an empty-net goal in the third. The Predators improved their record with the win, while the Kings fell to 5-9.

The Florida Panthers beat the Columbus Blue Jackets 4-1, and the St. Louis Blues edged the Detroit Red Wings 3-2, with Jimmy Snuggerud scoring the game-winner with just over four minutes left in the third period. The Panthers improved to 10-7-2, while the Blues moved to 8-8-2.

The night belonged to Winnipeg, though. A team that had lost nine straight, a fan base that had turned on its former goaltender, and a player in Mercer who scored twice against his old club: it was the kind of night that reminds you why the NHL's rivalries and returns matter so much. Hellebuyck will get another chance at his old team, and the Jets will try to keep their streak alive, but Tuesday belonged to the home crowd and to Dawson Mercer.
