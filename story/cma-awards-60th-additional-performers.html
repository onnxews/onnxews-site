Luke Combs, Chris Stapleton and More Join the 60th CMA Awards Lineup

Luke Combs, Vince Gill, Megan Moroney, Mumford and Sons and Chris Stapleton join the lineup for the milestone 60th show on November 18.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

The Country Music Association and ABC have announced the next round of performers for the 60th Annual CMA Awards, adding Luke Combs, Vince Gill, Megan Moroney, Mumford and Sons, Chris Stapleton, host Lainey Wilson and Stephen Wilson Jr. to a lineup that was already stacked. The announcement was made on Tuesday, November 10, eight days before the show.

The new names join an already-announced first round of performers that includes Ella Langley, Tim McGraw and Miranda Lambert, plus Cody Johnson, Kacey Musgraves, Zach Top, The Red Clay Strays and Tucker Wetmore. The two rounds together give the milestone 60th show one of the deepest performance lineups in the ceremony's history.

The night will also include a tribute to this year's recipients of the CMA Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award, Brooks and Dunn. Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, HARDY and Marcus King will come together for a performance honoring the duo, whose career spans more than three decades and some of the most enduring hits in country music.

The show airs live from Nashville's Bridgestone Arena on Wednesday, November 18, from 8 to 11 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+, and will be available to stream the next day on Hulu. Lainey Wilson, who is also performing, will host the ceremony, known as Country Music's Biggest Night.

Luke Combs arrives as one of the night's most nominated artists. The three-time nominee is up for Entertainer of the Year, Album of the Year and Male Vocalist of the Year, and his performance will be one of the most watched of the evening. His album 'The Way I Am' is among the contenders for the night's biggest prizes.

Vince Gill will make a special appearance, bringing what the CMA called his legendary artistry and musicianship to the stage. Gill is a Country Music Hall of Fame member and a 12-time CMA Awards host, and his presence connects the 60th show to the ceremony's history in a way few other performers could.

The 2026 show marks the 60th anniversary of the CMA Awards, a milestone six decades in the making that the association says reflects country music's enduring reach and the CMA's role as the industry's collective voice. The ceremony has grown from a small industry banquet into one of the biggest nights on the American music calendar, broadcast live to millions.

Tickets for the show at Bridgestone Arena are limited, with only a small number remaining, according to the CMA. The demand reflects both the milestone anniversary and a lineup that spans generations, from established stars such as Stapleton and Gill to newer names such as Moroney, Stephen Wilson Jr. and Zach Top.

The announcement also reflects how country music has broadened its reach. Mumford and Sons, a British folk-rock band, will take the CMA stage, while Halestorm, a rock band, joins the Brooks and Dunn tribute. The genre's biggest night now regularly includes artists from outside its traditional boundaries, a sign of how far its audience has grown.

More presenters are expected to be revealed in the days leading up to the show, the CMA said. For now, the picture is clear: the 60th CMA Awards will be a celebration of the genre's past, with Gill and Brooks and Dunn, and its present, with Combs, Wilson, Moroney and Stapleton leading a lineup that could fill a festival on its own.
