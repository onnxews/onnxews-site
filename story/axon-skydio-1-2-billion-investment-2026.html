Axon Bets $1.2 Billion on Skydio Drones as AI Revenue Tops $100 Million

The Taser and body-camera maker reported $909 million in quarterly revenue and unveiled its largest investment ever, taking a 37 percent stake in the drone company Skydio.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 6, 2026 · 2 min readFacebookX

Axon Enterprise reported third-quarter revenue of $909 million, up 28 percent from a year earlier, and announced a $1.2 billion investment in drone maker Skydio, the largest investment in the company's history.

The investment takes Axon's fully diluted ownership in Skydio to 37 percent and makes Axon the exclusive public safety distributor for Skydio drones in the United States and Canada. Chief executive Patrick Smith said drones will soon be overhead at emergency scenes within seconds of a 911 call, well before any human could arrive.

Software and services revenue rose 43 percent to $437 million, and annual recurring revenue reached $1.8 billion, up 40 percent. Axon's AI products have now passed $100 million in annual recurring revenue, a milestone reached in less than two years since the company's AI offerings launched.

Axon also introduced the Axon Body 5, a body camera with 25 times the processing power of the previous generation and a dedicated lens for scanning documents such as driver's licenses. The company said one device now records the scene and reads the paperwork.

The company trimmed its full-year revenue guidance to between $3.61 billion and $3.63 billion, citing slower federal procurement that shifted about $130 million in expected revenue out of 2026. Future contracted bookings still hit a record $15.8 billion, up 39 percent year over year.

Reporting: The Motley Fool
