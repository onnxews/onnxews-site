Nicki Minaj's a Dumb Ass

From a Michigan rally stage to White House press secretary rumors in 24 hours — the Nicki Minaj political tour has officially jumped the shark.

By Peter Osibajo · Orbit News Network · Oct 26, 2026 · 3 min read

PORT HURON, Mich. — Nicki Minaj spent Sunday onstage with Donald Trump at a Michigan rally for Republican Senate candidate Mike Rogers and gubernatorial candidate John James, declaring she'll support him “forever and ever” — and by Monday, the White House communications director was floating her as the next press secretary.

“Ladies and gentlemen, I think we may have found the new White House Press Secretary!” White House director of communications Stephen Cheung posted on X, attaching a video of Minaj praising Trump's work ethic and business acumen. White House adviser Alex Bruesewitz chimed in: “Hey Barbz, what do we think about this idea??” Minaj herself quote-tweeted Cheung's post with American flag and nervous laughter emoji. The job has been vacant since Katie Zacharia quit after just five days.

It is not clear how serious anyone is — a White House spokesperson just referred reporters back to Cheung's post. But that's the point: the whole thing reads like a troll that escaped containment. The briefing room is where administrations go to be taken seriously, and the pitch here is a pop star whose political résumé is rally cameos and a years-long grudge against California Gov. Gavin Newsom.

At the rally she also told the crowd: “We don't want our children to be bombarded with sex changes, right? We want people to leave the children alone. Leave them out of it.” And Trump, introducing her, told the crowd he'd “looked at one hell of a beautiful a— before: Nicki Minaj” and “That's hard to miss. Stand up! That's hard to miss” — before admitting he'd gone off topic.

Newsom's office, which has sparred with Minaj all year — she calls him “Newscum” and blames him for ignoring her after swatting incidents at her Beverly Hills home — answered the press-secretary chatter with four words: “We can only pray.” On this one, hard to argue. The Barbz can have the rallies; the briefing room deserves better than a meme candidacy.
