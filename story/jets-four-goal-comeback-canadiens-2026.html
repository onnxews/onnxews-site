Jets Pull Off Latest Four-Goal Comeback in NHL History, Beating Canadiens 5-4 in OT

Winnipeg trailed 4-0 before scoring four goals in 2:06 late in the third period, then Dylan Samberg won it 3:30 into overtime at the Bell Centre.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 4, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

The Winnipeg Jets beat the Montreal Canadiens 5-4 in overtime on Tuesday, November 3, at the Bell Centre, completing one of the most remarkable comebacks in NHL history.

Winnipeg trailed 4-0 before scoring four goals in a span of 2 minutes and 6 seconds late in the third period. Field Level Media calls it the latest four-goal comeback in NHL history, eclipsing the Los Angeles Kings' mark from November 10, 2007.

Tyrel Bauer scored his first NHL goal with 2:39 remaining. Morgan Barron and Kyle Connor, twice, including the tying goal with 33 seconds left, completed the rally.

Defenseman Dylan Samberg scored 3:30 into overtime for the winner, capping a night that had looked lost with minutes to play.

The win snapped Winnipeg's nine-game winless streak (0-7-2) and was its first win since October 13. Montreal, which had won five in a row, was left stunned, despite two power-play goals from Cole Caufield.

Reporting: Field Level Media
