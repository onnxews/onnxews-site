About Orbit News Network

Orbit News Network is an independent news site, published daily by Peter Osibajo. We cover the biggest national, world, sports and culture stories — posted fast, hook first, with no dry headlines.

Every article on this site is an original summary written by Orbit News Network, with a link to the source reporting. We never republish full article text from other outlets.

Photos on this site are freely licensed images (public domain or Creative Commons, via Wikimedia Commons) unless otherwise credited on the article itself.
