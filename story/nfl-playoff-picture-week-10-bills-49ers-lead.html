NFL Playoff Picture: Bills and 49ers Lead Their Conferences Entering Week 10

The Bills lead the AFC with a 49.1 percent chance at the No. 1 seed, while the 49ers top the NFC and the Packers face the week's biggest swing.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 11, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

The NFL playoff picture is taking shape, and the standings only tell part of the story. That is the conclusion of Pro Football Focus's Week 10 playoff picture, published November 11, which pairs the current standings with each team's projected chances of reaching the postseason and the games that will swing those chances the most.

The Buffalo Bills top the AFC playoff picture. At 7-1, Buffalo holds a 49.1 percent chance of securing the conference's No. 1 seed and the first-round bye and home-field advantage that comes with it. The Bills have been the AFC's most complete team, and their grip on the top seed is the strongest in the conference.

Behind them, the Denver Broncos sit second at 7-2, holding the head-to-head tiebreaker over the Jacksonville Jaguars. The Jaguars, 6-2, are the three-seed and hold a firm lead in the AFC South, where the next-best record belongs to the Houston Texans at 3-5. The Indianapolis Colts are 2-7 and the Tennessee Titans are 1-7, leaving Jacksonville with a clear path to a division title if it holds its form.

The middle of the AFC is crowded. The Pittsburgh Steelers, Kansas City Chiefs and Cincinnati Bengals are all 6-2 alongside Jacksonville, with the Jaguars holding the tiebreaker over the Steelers on in-conference win percentage. The Chiefs and Bengals currently sit as wild-card teams. The New England Patriots, at 5-4, hold the seventh and final seed, while the Cleveland Browns are also 5-4 but sit on the outside looking in, with the Patriots holding the tiebreaker.

In the NFC, the San Francisco 49ers lead the conference, according to PFF's projections, topping a race that remains far more open than the AFC's. The standings tell only part of the story in both conferences, and the projections show how quickly a single result can change a team's outlook.

Week 10 carries major playoff implications, and no game swings a team's chances more than the Green Bay Packers' matchup. The Packers' playoff probability rises to 76.2 percent with a win but falls to 36.9 percent with a loss, a swing of 39.3 percentage points. The Minnesota Vikings and New York Giants also have major swings riding on their results this week, making Week 10 one of the most consequential of the season.

At the other end of the standings, the race for the No. 1 overall pick in the 2027 NFL Draft is taking shape. The Miami Dolphins hold a 36.2 percent chance of landing the top pick, ahead of the Tennessee Titans at 31.1 percent. For those teams, the rest of the season is about the future, not the postseason.

The Jaguars' position is a reminder of how quickly fortunes change in the NFL. A year ago, Jacksonville was an afterthought; now it is the three-seed in the AFC with a division lead and a favorable schedule. Up next is a road matchup against the Titans, a game the Jaguars will be expected to win as they push toward a home playoff game.

The playoff picture will keep moving. Nine weeks of football remain after Week 10, and the projections will shift with every result. But the shape of the race is now clear: Buffalo and San Francisco lead their conferences, the AFC's middle is a six-team logjam at 6-2 or better, and a handful of Week 10 games will decide which teams are playing for seeding in December and which are playing for draft position in April.
