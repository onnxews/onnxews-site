NRG Energy Triples Its Profit and Sells $4 Billion of Gas Plants to LS Power

The power company earned $4.68 a share, far above estimates, and agreed to sell 4.9 gigawatts of gas-fired generation to LS Power for $4 billion in cash.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 9, 2026 · 3 min readFacebookX

NRG Energy delivered a sharp third-quarter earnings beat on Monday and paired it with a $4 billion portfolio reset, agreeing to sell six natural-gas power plants to LS Power as it sharpens its focus on returning cash to shareholders.

The Houston-based power company reported GAAP net income of $561 million for the quarter ended September 30, more than triple the $152 million it earned a year earlier. GAAP earnings per basic share rose to $2.59, from $0.70 in the same quarter last year, and cash provided by operating activities increased to $1.097 billion from $484 million.

On an adjusted basis, the results were stronger still. Adjusted net income reached $983 million, adjusted earnings per share were $4.68, and adjusted EBITDA was $2.013 billion, up from $1.205 billion a year earlier. Analysts polled by FactSet had expected adjusted earnings of $3.35 a share. Revenue jumped 24 percent to $9.47 billion, topping Wall Street estimates of $8.42 billion.

NRG attributed the improvement primarily to contributions from the portfolio it acquired from LS Power, higher realized capacity prices in the East, and stronger margins in Texas, including favorable weather impacts. Free cash flow before growth investments rose to $1.242 billion, from $828 million a year earlier.

The earnings release landed alongside a separate announcement: NRG agreed to sell 4.9 gigawatts of natural-gas-fired generation to LS Power for $4 billion in cash. The transaction covers six generation assets and is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027, subject to customary conditions and approvals from the Hart-Scott-Rodino process, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission and the New York State Public Service Commission.

The deal will allow NRG to pay down debt and buy back more of its own stock, the company said. 'Today's announcement is about returning more to our shareholders and supporting future growth investments,' said Robert Gaudette, NRG's president and chief executive. 'Our 17,000 employees delivered strong third quarter results that reflect the depth of NRG, with our eight million customers and a substantial generation business that produce steady earnings and cash flows.'

NRG reaffirmed its full-year 2026 guidance, pointing to adjusted EBITDA of $5.325 billion to $5.825 billion and adjusted earnings per share of $7.90 to $9.90. It also initiated guidance for 2027, expecting adjusted earnings of $8.05 to $10.05 a share, compared with analyst views for $10.19 a share. The company said it expects to update both its financial guidance and its capital allocation plan after the sale closes.

The company also declared a dividend payable on November 16, 2026, to stockholders of record as of November 2. NRG closed at $88.29 on Friday, November 6, before the results, and hosted an earnings conference call at 8 a.m. Eastern on Monday to discuss the quarter.

The results cap a strong morning for American power companies, with Constellation Energy also beating estimates on Monday, as surging electricity demand from data centers and a tight power market lift the whole sector. For NRG, the $4 billion sale marks a deliberate shift: fewer gas plants, a stronger balance sheet, and more cash flowing back to shareholders.
