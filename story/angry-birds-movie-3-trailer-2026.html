'The Angry Birds Movie 3' Drops Its New Trailer, Ahead of a December 23 Release

Paramount's synopsis promises that one angry bird will face his greatest challenge yet: surviving fatherhood while saving the world. Jason Sudeikis returns as Red.

ONOrbit News NetworkNov 4, 2026 · 1 min readFacebookX

A new trailer for The Angry Birds Movie 3 was released on November 4, 2026, as announced on November 3 by ComingSoon.

Paramount's synopsis reads: "This holiday season, one angry bird will face his greatest challenge yet, surviving fatherhood, while saving the world!"

The film opens in theaters on December 23, 2026, after being moved forward from January 29, 2027, giving it a prime holiday-season slot.

The film is directed by John Rice, from a screenplay by Thurop Van Orman. Jason Sudeikis returns as Red, with Danny McBride, Josh Gad and Rachel Bloom returning alongside him.

The trailer launch kicks off the final marketing push for a franchise that has already spanned two films, a streaming series and a vast merchandising line.
